## Where you can get passport photos taken

In the US, the State Department says you can get a passport photo from an acceptance facility that takes photos, "any company that offers photo services," or a friend or family member. In practice, most people use one of these:

| Type of place | Examples | Good for | Check before you go |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pharmacies** | [CVS](/tool/cvs-passport-photos), [Walgreens](/tool/walgreens-passport-photos) | Late opening hours, many locations | Whether the photo counter is staffed when you visit |
| **Big-box and office stores** | [Walmart](/tool/walmart-passport-photos), [Staples](/tool/staples-passport-photos) | Combining the trip with other shopping | Not every store offers the service |
| **Shipping and print stores** | [FedEx Office](/tool/fedex-office-passport-photos), The UPS Store | Digital copies and reprints | Many are independently run, so services vary |
| **Post offices** | [USPS](/tool/usps-post-office-passport-photos) passport locations | Taking the photo and applying in one visit | Many need an appointment; not all take photos |
| **Photo studios** | Local photographers | Babies, retakes, special requirements | Price and turnaround time |

A few chains you may remember no longer offer this. Rite Aid has closed its stores, and Costco closed its in-store photo centres. See our list of [other stores that take passport photos](/tool/other-stores-that-take-passport-photos) for smaller chains.

Prices change often and vary by location, so we do not list them here. Our [comparison of passport photo prices](/tool/passport-photo-cost) explains what drives the cost and how the options compare.

## Use the official locator to apply and get photos in one trip

If you also need to submit a passport application in person, start with the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/). Enter your ZIP code to see nearby post offices, clerks of court, libraries and other facilities that accept applications. Many list whether they also take passport photos.

Applying and getting your photo in the same visit saves a trip, but it is not always the cheapest option, and appointment slots can fill up weeks ahead in busy seasons.

## What to check before you go

A two-minute phone call or a look at the store's page can save a wasted trip:

1. **Is the photo service available at this location today?** Store locators often list it, but staff training and equipment vary.
2. **Do you need an appointment?** Post offices often require one, and stores usually do not.
3. **Will you get prints, a digital file, or both?** If you are renewing online, you need a digital photo, not a print.
4. **Can they size the photo for another country?** Many US stores only print 2×2 inch photos. For a 35 × 45 mm or other size, ask first.
5. **How many copies do you get?** A US passport application needs one photo. Some other forms, such as many immigration applications, ask for two.

## How to make sure the photo passes

The person taking your photo is not checking it for the State Department, so it is worth checking yourself before you leave:

- Plain white or off-white background with no shadows
- Head straight, both eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression
- No glasses, and no hat unless it is religious or medical
- Head size between 1 and 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm), chin to top of hair
- A sharp print on photo paper, not a photocopy

If anything looks wrong, ask for a retake on the spot. The full list is in our [passport photo rules checklist](/tool/passport-photo-requirements).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I get passport photos taken right now?

Pharmacies and big-box stores are usually the best bet for a same-day, walk-in photo, because they have long opening hours and do not need an appointment. Call ahead to check that someone who can take photos is working when you plan to go.

### Do post offices take passport photos?

Many post offices that accept passport applications also take photos, but not all of them, and most need an appointment. Use the [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/) or call your local post office to check.

### Can I get a digital passport photo at a store?

Some stores give you a digital copy on request, and some do not. If you are renewing online, ask before you pay, or take the photo at home and prepare the digital file yourself.

### What if there is nowhere near me that takes passport photos?

You can take the photo at home with a phone against a plain white or off-white wall, then have it sized and printed. Any photo kiosk can print a 6×4″ sheet with several passport photos on it.

### Are passport photo booths accepted?

Booth photos can be accepted if they meet every rule, including size, background and image quality. Check the print before you leave, as booth photos are more often too dark or the wrong size.
