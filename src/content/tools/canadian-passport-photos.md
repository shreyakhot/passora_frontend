## Canadian passport photo size and specs

You need **2 identical photos** with each paper application.

| Rule | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Size | 50 mm wide × 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ inches) |
| Head size | 31–36 mm (1¼ to 1⁷⁄₁₆ inches), chin to crown |
| Background | Plain white or light-coloured, clearly contrasting with the face |
| Colour | Colour or black and white |
| Age | Taken no more than 6 months before you apply |
| Print | Professionally printed on plain, high-quality photo paper |
| Edits | None: no software, filters, AI, colour changes, cropping or background changes |

**Pose and expression:** face and shoulders centred and square to the camera, eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression (neither smiling nor frowning). Hair must be off the face so its edges are visible.

**Glasses** are allowed if your eyes are clearly visible and there is no glare. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are not, even with a prescription. Head coverings are allowed only if worn daily for religious or medical reasons, with the full face visible.

**Babies:** a car seat photo is allowed with a plain white blanket behind the head and no shadows. Hands must not appear in a child's photo. Our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) has tips for getting the shot.

### Digital photo for online renewal

If you renew online, you upload a digital photo instead. It must also be taken by a commercial photographer, in person; a scanned print is not accepted.

| Rule | Digital photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Shape | Portrait, 3:2 aspect ratio |
| Pixels | Between 1,800 × 1,200 and 4,500 × 3,000 |
| File | JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg), 200 KB to 5 MB, saved straight from the camera |
| Head size | 45–50% of the photo's height, chin to crown |

You also enter the studio's name and address and the date the photo was taken. Ask the photographer for a digital file and prints at the same visit, in case you cannot renew online.

### Photos of babies and young children

The same size and photographer rules apply, with some leeway on expression. The Government of Canada says "Newborns may have a range of facial expressions" and "We do allow some range for infant facial expressions." The photo must still show your child's face and shoulders centred and squared to the camera, not tilted in any direction, and it must not show your hands or your child's hands. For a newborn photographed in a car seat, a white blanket is placed over the seat behind the child's head. Our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) has more tips for photographing babies.

## Photographer requirements

Canada is one of the few countries that requires a **commercial photographer or photo studio**. On the back of each printed photo:

- **The photographer** writes or stamps the date it was taken and the studio's name and complete address.
- **Your guarantor** writes a statement certifying it is a true likeness of you, with your name, and signs it. Adult renewals do not need a guarantor.

This is why photos taken at home, in a booth or on your own phone are not accepted for Canadian passports, even if the size is right.

## Where to get Canadian passport photos

### Near me

In Canada, pharmacies, photo shops and independent studios take passport photos. Shoppers Drug Mart, for example, says its stores take passport photos with **no appointment**, and also photos for PR cards, citizenship applications and travel visas.

Wherever you go, check these before you pay:

- They know the **Canadian** passport size (50 × 70 mm), not the US 2 × 2 inch one.
- They **stamp or write** the studio's name, address and date on the back.
- They can give you a **digital file** if you plan to renew online.
- You check the photos before you leave: head size, no shadows, no glare on glasses.

Costco has changed its photo services in recent years; see our [guide to Costco photo options](/tool/costco-passport-photos), including Canada. If you are in the US and need a Canadian passport photo, ask for the Canadian size by name, and make sure the store can add its details and the date on the back.

## Canadian citizenship photos

IRCC says citizenship photos **do not use the same specifications as passport photos**, so ask for citizenship photos by name. For a citizenship grant (becoming a citizen) by mail:

| Rule | Citizenship photo (paper application) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Number | 2 identical colour prints |
| Size | 50 mm wide × 70 mm high |
| Head size | 31–36 mm, chin to crown |
| Background | Plain white or light-coloured |
| Photographer | Commercial photographer or studio |
| Back of the photo | Your first and last name, the studio's name and full address, the date taken |
| Age | Taken no more than 6 months before you apply |

The colour requirement is one difference: passport photos may be black and white, citizenship photos may not. Another is the back of the photo, which carries **your name** and needs no guarantor. Do not staple or glue the photos to the form.

**Online citizenship applications** need a digital photo from a commercial photographer: JPEG with minimal compression, at least 420 × 540 pixels, about 240 KB and no more than 4 MB, unedited.

### Citizenship card with photo

Canada stopped issuing citizenship cards, which had a photo, in February 2012. A card you already have is still valid proof of citizenship. Proof of citizenship is now a citizenship certificate, which does not show a photo. The application for a certificate still asks for photos, though, with its own IRCC specifications, so follow the instructions for that application.

## Canada visitor visa photo requirements

Visitors to Canada use a different, smaller photo.

| Document | Size | Head size | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Visitor visa (temporary resident visa) | At least 35 × 45 mm | 31–36 mm | 2 identical photos, taken in the last 6 months; clear prescription glasses allowed |
| Permanent resident card | 50 × 70 mm | 31–36 mm | Taken in the last 12 months; digital 715 × 1000 to 2000 × 2800 pixels, up to 4 MB |

Most visitor visa applications are made online; follow the photo instructions in your online account. For how Canada compares with other countries, see our [guide to visa photo rules](/tool/visa-photo).

## Cost

Prices for Canadian passport photos are set by each pharmacy or studio and were not confirmed for this guide. Ask for the price of:

- Two printed photos with the stamp on the back
- A digital file for online renewal, if you need one
- Citizenship or PR photos, if different

Some studios charge extra for babies or for retakes. Check before the photo is taken.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size is a Canadian passport photo?

50 mm wide by 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ inches), with the head 31–36 mm from chin to crown.

### Can I take my own Canadian passport photo?

No. Canadian passport photos must be taken by a commercial photographer or photo studio, which writes its name, address and the date on the back.

### Can I smile in a Canadian passport photo?

No. The expression must be neutral, with the mouth closed, neither smiling nor frowning. Newborns are allowed some expression.

### Are glasses allowed in Canadian passport photos?

Yes, if your eyes are clearly visible and there is no glare. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are not allowed.

### Are citizenship photos the same as passport photos?

No. IRCC says they use different specifications. Citizenship photos must be in colour and have your name, the studio's details and the date on the back.
