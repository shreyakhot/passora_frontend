## Does Walgreens do passport photos?

Yes. Walgreens says passport photos are "available in-store only at most of our Walgreens locations." Not every store offers them, so check before you go (see below). You cannot order Walgreens passport photos online or have them made from a photo you upload.

Walgreens takes photos for all ages, including babies, and lists passport and visa photos for around 30 other countries, such as Canada, China, India, Mexico and the UK. For anything else, it says to "consult your local store."

## Walgreens passport photo price

On **November 6, 2026**, Walgreens' [passport photos page](https://photo.walgreens.com/store/passport-photos) listed **$16.99** for two 2 × 2 inch printed photos. Prices can change, so check the page or ask in store.

That price includes a free digital copy by email. A copy on a USB drive costs extra; Walgreens does not list that price online.

For a side-by-side look at other stores and cheaper routes, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## How to get a passport photo at Walgreens

### In store

1. Go to the photo counter and ask a staff member. Walgreens says there is "no appointment needed."
2. The staff member takes your photo, and the kiosk checks it with pattern recognition software.
3. You get two 2 × 2 inch prints, "typically available in a few minutes."

Walgreens describes its photos as "guaranteed to be government compliant." Its policy page does not set out retake or refund terms, so ask in store what happens if a photo is rejected, and check the print yourself before you leave (see below).

### The free digital copy

The emailed digital copy is useful if you are renewing a US passport online:

- **Ask for it when you order.** It has to be selected at the time of the order and cannot be added later.
- **Bring your phone.** You enter your email address and mobile number at the kiosk. Walgreens emails a link, and a verification code is sent by text when you open it.
- **Download it promptly.** Walgreens says the link expires 30 days after the order.
- **Do not edit it.** Walgreens notes that the State Department's renewal system needs an unedited image. Upload the file as you received it.

Our guide to [getting a digital passport photo](/tool/digital-passport-photo) explains what the online renewal system accepts.

### 2 × 2 prints from your own photo

Walgreens' passport photo service is in store only, and its photo site does not offer a passport product for your own uploaded photo. If you already have a correctly sized photo, you can print it as a standard 4 × 6 print instead; our guide to [printing passport photos at home or in store](/tool/print-passport-photos) shows how to lay out the sheet.

## Duane Reade passport photos

Duane Reade is owned by Walgreens, and Duane Reade stores in New York City appear in Walgreens' own passport photo store finder. Walgreens does not say whether the price and service are exactly the same, so call the store to confirm before you go.

## Hours and locations

Use the [Walgreens store locator](https://www.walgreens.com/storelocator/find.jsp) and choose **Passport Photos** under the Photo filters. Walgreens also has city pages that list stores with passport photos.

The photo counter must be staffed to take your photo, and photo hours can be shorter than pharmacy hours. Phone the store before you go early in the morning, late at night or at the weekend.

Walgreens is closing some stores as part of a long-term plan, so the store you remember may have closed. Check the locator rather than relying on an old address.

## Check your Walgreens photo before you leave

- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
- Both eyes open, neutral expression, mouth closed, looking at the camera
- No glasses; no hat unless it is religious or medical
- Head between 1 and 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm), chin to top of hair
- Sharp print, no blur or grain

If something looks wrong, ask for a retake while you are there. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an appointment for a Walgreens passport photo?

No. Walgreens says no appointment is needed; go to the photo counter and ask a member of staff.

### Is the Walgreens digital passport photo free?

Yes, with the purchase of a passport photo. It is emailed to you, but you must choose it when you order, and the download link expires after 30 days.

### Can I use a Walgreens photo to renew my passport online?

Yes, use the emailed digital copy. Upload it unedited, and check that it was taken within the last six months.

### Can Walgreens take a passport photo of a baby?

Walgreens takes passport photos for all ages, including babies. A busy counter can be hard with a newborn; our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) has tips and alternatives.

### Does Walgreens accept passport applications?

Walgreens' service covers the photo only. To apply in person, use the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/).
