## Where to get passport size photos printed nearby

### Photo studios

A local studio takes the photo and prints it in the size you ask for, usually while you wait. Studios are the safest choice when you need an unusual size, a set of copies for several forms, or a photo of a baby. See our guide to [booking a professional photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me).

### Print shops and copy centres

Many print and copy shops take passport photos and print sets of copies. Shops near passport offices, embassies and universities often know local form requirements well. Ask whether they can print sizes other than the one they usually sell.

### Retail photo counters

In the US, pharmacies and big-box stores take passport photos, but they usually print only the US 2 × 2 inch size. For example, Walmart says "all photos are printed as 2×2 photo prints." If you need 35 × 45 mm or another size, ask before you pay. Our guide to [nearby places that take passport photos](/tool/passport-photos-near-me) lists the main US chains.

### Photo booths

Booths are fast and cheap, and in the UK many give you a code to use with an online passport application. Check the print before you leave: booth photos are more often too dark, blurred or framed badly.

## Which size to ask for

"Passport size" means different things in different countries, so name the document, not just "passport size":

| Document | Size to ask for |
| :--- | :--- |
| US passport or US visa | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |
| UK passport | 35 × 45 mm |
| Schengen visa | 35 × 45 mm |
| Canadian passport | 50 × 70 mm |
| Exam, job or school forms | Check the form; often 35 × 45 mm in India and the UK |

Our guide to [sizes in inches, millimetres and pixels](/tool/passport-photo-size) explains how much of the frame your head should fill. For the metric standard, see the [35 × 45 mm photo guide](/tool/35x45mm-passport-photo).

## Photo sets: 4, 6 and 8 copies

Many shops sell passport size photos as a set printed on one sheet and cut for you:

- **4 copies:** enough for a passport plus a spare
- **6 copies:** common for visa applications that need two photos, plus spares
- **8 copies:** useful for exam forms, ID cards and admission paperwork where several offices each ask for one

One standard 6 × 4 in (15 × 10 cm) photo print holds several passport size photos. Buying a sheet with more copies than you need is usually cheaper than coming back later. A photo for an official passport should be recent, so do not use old spares for a new passport.

## What passport size photos cost

Prices depend on the shop, the city, the number of copies and whether a digital file is included, so we do not list them. Store chains in the US usually sell a fixed pack of two prints, while studios and print shops elsewhere often price by the set. ## Print passport size photos from a digital file

If you already have a suitable photo, you do not need to have it taken again. You only need it printed at the right size:

1. Start with a sharp, recent photo taken against a plain white or off-white wall, facing the camera.
2. Crop it to the size for your document, with the head measured correctly in the frame.
3. Lay out several copies on a 6 × 4 in sheet at 300 dpi.
4. Print it at a photo kiosk, a print shop or at home on photo paper, choosing the 6 × 4 in size with no scaling ("actual size").
5. Cut along the edges and check one photo with a ruler.

The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) does steps 2 and 3 for you and gives you a print-ready sheet. Our guide to [printing passport photos at home or in store](/tool/print-passport-photos) covers paper and printer settings.

For a US passport, start from a photo that already has a plain white or off-white background. The US State Department rejects photos changed with software, apps or filters, so only crop and resize; do not edit the background.

## Passport size photos in India, the UK and Canada

**India.** "Passport size photo" or "PP size photo" is the everyday name for small ID prints needed for passports, visas, exams, bank accounts and admissions. Many forms set their own size and background, so read the form before you go to the studio.

**United Kingdom.** UK passport photos are 35 × 45 mm. For an online application, GOV.UK says to "get a digital passport photo from a photo booth or shop" and choose the option to get a code with your photos, which you enter in your application. Photos must be "unaltered by computer software."

**Canada.** Canadian passport photos are 50 × 70 mm, and the Government of Canada says to "go to a commercial photographer or photo studio" to have them taken. The studio's name, full address and the date go on the back of one photo. A US-style 2 × 2 print will not do.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many passport size photos do I need?

It depends on the form. A US passport application needs one photo, many visa applications ask for two, and exam or admission forms often ask for one each. Check every form before you order, and get a few spares.

### Can I get passport size photos printed from a photo on my phone?

Yes, if the photo meets the rules for your document: recent, sharp, facing the camera, against a plain light background and not edited. Crop and lay it out at the right size, then print it at any photo kiosk or print shop.

### Is a passport size photo the same as a 2 × 2 inch photo?

Only in the US. The UK, India, the Schengen countries and many others use 35 × 45 mm, and Canada uses 50 × 70 mm. Always ask for the size by document.

### Can I use the same passport size photos for different documents?

Only if the documents use the same size and rules. A US passport photo will not work for a UK passport or a Schengen visa, and many forms require a photo taken in the last six months.
