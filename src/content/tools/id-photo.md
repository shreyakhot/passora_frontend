## What is an ID photo?

An ID photo is a recent photo of your head and shoulders, taken straight on against a plain background, that identifies you on a document. "Passport photo" and "ID photo" are often used to mean the same thing, and phrases like "passport ID photo" or "passport photo ID" usually mean one of two things: a photo for a passport, or a passport-style photo for another ID.

The difference matters because ID documents work in two ways:

- **You bring the photo.** Passports, many visas and some immigration forms ask for printed photos in an exact size, or a digital file.
- **The office takes the photo.** Driver's licences, state ID cards and trusted traveller cards are usually photographed when you apply, so you bring nothing.

Check which kind your document is before you pay for photos.

## ID photo vs passport photo

| | Passport photo | Other ID photos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Who takes it | You, a store or a studio | Often the issuing office, at your appointment |
| Size | Exact, set by the country (2 × 2 in for the US) | Set by the issuer; often you do not need to know it |
| Rules | Strict: background, head size, expression, no glasses for the US | Similar in spirit, sometimes less strict |
| Editing | Not allowed | Usually not allowed for government IDs |

A passport photo meets the strictest version of the rules, so a spare US passport photo often works for another document that asks for a 2 × 2 inch "passport-style" photo. It does not work the other way round: a photo made for a student card or work badge is rarely good enough for a passport.

## Common ID photo sizes

| Document | Photo size |
| :--- | :--- |
| US passport book and passport card | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |
| US visa, green card and work permit forms | 2 × 2 in |
| UK, Schengen and many other passports | 35 × 45 mm |
| Canadian passport | 50 × 70 mm |
| Driver's licence and state ID | Taken at the office |
| Student and staff ID | Set by the school or employer, often a digital upload |

The US passport card uses the same application form as the passport book, Form DS-11, which says: "Attach one photo, 2x2 inches in size." One photo covers both if you apply for them together.

**ID card size** is a different measure: it is the size of the card, not the photo. Most plastic ID cards, including many national ID cards and driver's licences, use the ID-1 format of 85.60 × 53.98 mm, the size of a bank card. The issuer prints your photo on the card at its own size, so you never need an "ID card size photo" yourself.

For passports in other countries, our [list of photo sizes for every country](/tool/passport-photo-size-by-country) gives each one, and our [guide to passport photo dimensions](/tool/passport-photo-size) explains inches, millimetres and pixels.

## Documents that need ID photos

| Document (US) | Do you bring a photo? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Passport book or card | Yes: one 2 × 2 in photo, or a digital photo for online renewal |
| Driver's licence or state ID | No: the DMV takes it. California's renewal steps include "Have your photo taken," and New York says "You must go to a DMV office to change your photo." |
| TSA PreCheck | No: the enrollment provider takes your fingerprints and photo |
| Global Entry | No: the photo is taken at your interview |
| TWIC card | No: you are fingerprinted and photographed at enrollment |
| Work permit (EAD) | Yes: two 2 × 2 in photos with a paper Form I-765, or an upload if you file online |
| Green card | Photos with some forms; the card photo is taken at your biometrics appointment |
| Immigrant visa | Yes: two identical photos at the interview |
| School, college or work ID | Depends: often taken on site or uploaded online |

Rules change and differ by state, so check the instructions for your document. Our [guide to USCIS photo rules](/tool/uscis-immigration-photos) covers green cards and work permits in detail, and our [visa photo guide](/tool/visa-photo) covers visas.

## Where to get ID photos near you

Most places that take passport photos also take other ID photos:

- **Pharmacies, shipping stores and post offices** take 2 × 2 inch photos while you wait.
- **Photo studios** can take any size, including 35 × 45 mm and other countries' formats, and are the best choice for an unusual size or a digital file in a set format. Our [guide to choosing a photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me) has a checklist.
- **Photo booths** are common in some countries and print several photos at once.

Ask for the document by name, such as "a US visa photo" or "two 35 × 45 mm photos", rather than "an ID photo". Many store services are set up for US passport photos, so ask whether they can do another size before you pay. Our [guide to places that take passport photos](/tool/passport-photos-near-me) lists the main chains and how to find a location.

## Take an ID photo yourself

For a document that accepts a photo you supply, you can take it at home:

1. Stand about 2 feet (60 cm) in front of a plain white or off-white wall, facing a window.
2. Have someone else take the photo from about 4 feet (1.2 m) away, with the camera at eye level.
3. Keep a neutral expression, eyes open, and remove hats and glasses.
4. Crop the photo to the size the document asks for, without editing your face or the background.
5. Print it on photo paper at 100% scale, or upload the file if the form allows it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size is an ID photo?

It depends on the document. US passports, visas and immigration forms use 2 × 2 inches; the UK and much of Europe use 35 × 45 mm. Driver's licences and many other cards are photographed at the office, so you do not need to know a size.

### Is an ID photo the same as a passport photo?

Not always. A passport photo is the strictest kind of ID photo. A spare US passport photo usually works for another document that asks for a 2 × 2 inch passport-style photo, but a photo for a work or student badge is rarely good enough for a passport.

### Do I need to bring a photo to the DMV?

Usually not. DMV offices take your photo when you apply or renew in person. Check your state's instructions if you renew by mail from out of state, as some forms ask for a printed photo.

### Can I use my driver's licence photo for my passport?

No. A passport needs its own photo that meets the passport rules and was taken in the last six months.

### Where can I get ID photos printed?

At most pharmacy photo counters, shipping stores and photo studios. If you already have a correctly sized photo, you can also print it on a 4 × 6 sheet at a photo kiosk.
