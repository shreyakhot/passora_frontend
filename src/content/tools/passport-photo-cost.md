### Pharmacy chains

Pharmacies sit in the middle of the price range. The main difference is the digital copy: [Walgreens](/tool/walgreens-passport-photos) emails one free with your purchase, while [CVS](/tool/cvs-passport-photos) charges extra and offers it only at select stores.

### Shipping and office stores

FedEx Office charges one price for either prints or a USB copy, not both. UPS Store prices are set by each franchise, so phone your local store. Staples sells prints, a digital file and a bundle as separate options.

### Post office

The USPS lists a $15.00 photo fee at post offices that take photos. If you are also applying in person, the post office acceptance fee ($35.00, listed separately by USPS) and the government's passport fees are on top.

### Warehouse clubs

Costco closed its in-store photo centres in 2021, so it no longer takes passport photos. See our list of [other stores that take passport photos](/tool/other-stores-that-take-passport-photos).

### Studios

Studios charge the most, especially for extras such as digital files, more prints or a home visit. You pay for the time and care, which is often worth it for babies or unusual requirements. See our guide to [choosing a professional photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me).

### Online and DIY

Taking the photo yourself costs only the print. If you need help with the measuring and cropping, an online tool charges for the finished files, usually a digital photo and a print sheet. The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) shows its price before you pay.

## The cheapest ways to get a passport photo

### Free options

- **AAA membership:** some AAA clubs include passport photos with higher membership tiers. For example, AAA Club Alliance lists photos as free for Premier members, $8.99 for Plus, $11.99 for Classic and $14.99 for non-members. Other clubs set their own prices.
- **A friend with a camera:** the State Department accepts photos taken by anyone, as long as they meet the rules.

### DIY at home

1. Take the photo against a plain white or off-white wall in daylight.
2. Crop it to 2 × 2 inches with the head at the right size.
3. Lay out a 4 × 6 inch sheet and print it at a photo kiosk.

One 4 × 6 print usually costs far less than any store photo. Our guides show how to [set up the photo at home](/tool/passport-photo-at-home) and [print it at the right size](/tool/print-passport-photos).

### Memberships and coupons

Check membership discounts (such as AAA) before you go. If you have a store photo coupon, read the terms to see whether passport photos are included.

## What affects the price

- **Prints only or a digital copy too:** some stores include the digital file, others charge extra or offer only a USB drive
- **Number of copies:** most stores sell a fixed pack of two
- **Other countries:** non-US sizes can cost more
- **Location:** franchise stores and studios set their own prices
- **Extras:** retakes, home visits and rush service

## Is a cheap passport photo a false saving?

A photo that is rejected can cost far more than the photo itself: a delayed application, a new photo, and possibly a missed trip. Whatever you pay, check the photo against the rules before you send it:

- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
- Head between 1 and 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm)
- Eyes open, neutral expression, no glasses
- Sharp, on photo paper, unedited

A cheap photo that meets every rule is just as good as an expensive one. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list.

## Passport photo cost for kids and babies

Most stores charge the same for a child as for an adult. Babies are where costs can rise, because a newborn may need several attempts, and a studio charges more for the time. Taking the photo at home with the baby on a white sheet is the cheapest option; our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) explains how.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is a passport photo at the post office?

USPS lists a $15.00 photo fee at post offices that take photos (checked November 6, 2026). Not every post office takes photos, and many need an appointment.

### What is the cheapest place to get a passport photo?

Taking the photo yourself and printing it at a photo kiosk is usually cheapest. Among stores, Walmart had the lowest listed price we found, at $7.64 on November 6, 2026.

### Are passport photo fees included in the passport fee?

No. The photo is paid to whoever takes it. Passport application fees are paid to the US Department of State, and an acceptance facility charges its own acceptance fee.

### Do I have to pay for a digital passport photo?

It depends on the store. Walgreens includes a free emailed copy with its photos, CVS charges extra, and FedEx Office offers a USB copy instead of prints for the same price.
