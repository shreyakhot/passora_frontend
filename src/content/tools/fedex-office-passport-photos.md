## Does FedEx Office do passport photos?

Yes. FedEx says passport photo services are available at any FedEx Office location nationwide, with **no appointment needed**. A team member takes the photo, and FedEx says its software checks it against the other official requirements. Photos are ready in minutes.

FedEx Office also takes photos for other countries' passports and visas. Tell the team member which country and document the photo is for.

## FedEx passport photo cost

On **November 11, 2026**, a FedEx Office store page listed passport photos at **$15.95**: "choose between two 2" x 2" printed photos or a digital copy provided on a convenient USB drive." FedEx's passport services page lists visa photos from the same price.

Note the "or": the price covers prints **or** a USB copy, not both. Prices can vary by store, so check with your local store. For other stores' prices, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## FedEx vs FedEx Office vs Kinko's

- **Kinko's** was a chain of copy and print shops. FedEx bought it in 2004.
- **FedEx Kinko's** was the stores' name after the purchase.
- **FedEx Office** has been the name since 2008. These are the stores that take passport photos.

Other FedEx locations, such as FedEx Ship Centers and FedEx counters inside other shops, are mainly for shipping. Look for a FedEx Office store when you need a photo.

## Locations and hours

Find a store with the FedEx Office store locator, which lists each store's hours and services. Each store also has its own passport photo page.

The photo service needs a team member free to take it. Phone ahead if you are going at a busy time or near closing.

## Can you renew a passport at FedEx?

Not directly. FedEx says: "FedEx Office is not a passport acceptance facility and does not issue passports or other travel documents." What it offers:

- **The photo.** Get the prints for a renewal by mail, or the USB copy for an online renewal.
- **A partner service.** FedEx offers paid help with passport applications through RushMyPassport, a private expediting company. Its service fees are on top of the government fees.
- **Not mailing a DS-82.** The State Department's address for renewals by mail is a PO box, and it says not to use UPS, FedEx or DHL. Send the application by a trackable mail service instead.

For how each renewal route works and how to attach the photo, see our guide to [photos for renewals and new applications](/tool/passport-application-photo). Our guide to [the different kinds of passport photo and application services](/tool/passport-photo-services) explains what an expediting company can and cannot speed up.

## Check your FedEx photo before you leave

- 2 × 2 inches, head 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair
- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
- Eyes open, neutral expression, no glasses
- Sharp print with no blur or grain

If something looks wrong, ask for a retake while you are there. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is a passport photo at FedEx?

A FedEx Office store page listed $15.95 on November 11, 2026, for two 2 × 2 inch prints or a digital copy on a USB drive. Check with your local store.

### Do I need an appointment at FedEx Office?

No. FedEx says no appointment is needed; walk in during store hours.

### Can FedEx give me a digital passport photo?

Yes, on a USB drive, instead of prints for the same price. Use it for online renewal, uploaded without edits.

### Is Kinko's the same as FedEx Office?

Yes. FedEx bought Kinko's in 2004, and the stores have been called FedEx Office since 2008.

### Can I mail my passport renewal with FedEx?

The State Department says not to, because the renewal address is a PO box. Use a trackable mail service instead.
