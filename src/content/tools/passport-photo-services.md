## The four types of passport photo services

| Service type | What you get | Typical places | Best when |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Photo only** | Prints, sometimes a digital file | Pharmacies, big-box stores, print shops, studios | You will mail or upload the application yourself |
| **Photo + application acceptance** | Photo and an official acceptance of your application | Many post offices and other acceptance facilities | A first passport, a child's passport, or any in-person application |
| **Online photo check and formatting** | Your own photo cropped to size, a digital file and a print sheet | Online photo tools | You can take a good photo at home and want no trip |
| **Expedited passport services** | A private company submits your application or picks up your passport | Courier and expediting companies, some in retail partnerships | You have urgent travel and cannot get to a passport agency |

### Photo only

Most people get their passport photo this way. Pharmacies such as [CVS](/tool/cvs-passport-photos) and [Walgreens](/tool/walgreens-passport-photos), big-box stores such as [Walmart](/tool/walmart-passport-photos) and print chains such as [Staples](/tool/staples-passport-photos) take the photo while you wait. A studio takes more care, which matters for babies and special cases; see our guide to [choosing a professional photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me).

### Photo + passport application acceptance

If you must apply in person, for example for a first adult passport or any child's passport, an acceptance facility checks your documents and seals your application. Many post offices and some other facilities also take photos, so you can do both in one trip. Use the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/) to find one, and check whether it needs an appointment.

### Online photo check and formatting

You take the photo at home and upload it. An online tool finds your face, sizes your head to your country's rules and crops the photo, then gives you a digital file and a sheet to print. The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) works this way. For a US passport it only crops and sizes the photo and does not edit the background, because the State Department rejects photos changed with software.

### Expedited passport services

These are private companies, sometimes called couriers or expeditors. The State Department says they charge extra fees, and that using one "won't get you your passport faster" than applying at a passport agency yourself. The usual reason to use one is urgent travel when you cannot get to an agency.

## Where to get a passport photo taken: by situation

### You need it today

A walk-in store photo counter or an online tool is fastest. Phone a store first to check the photo counter is staffed. Our [same-day passport photo guide](/tool/same-day-instant-passport-photos) ranks the options by speed.

### You want the lowest price

Taking the photo at home and printing a sheet at a kiosk is usually cheapest. Our [comparison of passport photo prices](/tool/passport-photo-cost) covers the trade-offs.

### You need a digital copy

For online renewal you need a digital file, not a print. Some stores offer a digital copy for an extra fee or on a USB drive; online tools give you one by default. See our guide to [getting a digital passport photo](/tool/digital-passport-photo).

### You have a baby or toddler

A studio or a photo taken at home, with the baby lying on a plain white sheet, usually works better than a busy store counter. Our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) explains the rules.

## What each service should include

### Prints

A US passport application needs one 2 × 2 inch photo; stores usually give you two. Check the print before you leave: plain white or off-white background, no shadows, eyes open, head between 1 and 1 3/8 inches.

### A digital file

Ask whether it is included or extra, and how you receive it (email or USB drive).

### A retake or acceptance promise

Some services promise a free retake if your photo is rejected. Read what it covers. No company can guarantee acceptance, because only the passport authority decides.

## How to avoid scams and overpriced middlemen

- **Appointments at passport agencies are free.** The State Department says it does not charge to make appointments, and that a request to pay for one should be treated as fraud. Book by calling 877-487-2778.
- **Urgent travel has an official route.** Passport agencies take appointments if you travel abroad within 14 days, or need a foreign visa within 28 days.
- **Watch for look-alike seals.** The State Department warns that some companies use logos that look like official government seals. Private companies are not part of the government.
- **Check registration.** If you do use a courier, check that it is registered with the State Department before you pay.
- **Photo fees are separate from passport fees.** A photo service charges for the photo. Government passport fees are paid to the US Department of State.

## Getting the photo and application done in one trip

1. Find an acceptance facility that also takes photos with the [official locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/).
2. Book an appointment if it needs one.
3. Bring your completed form (unsigned, if it is Form DS-11), proof of citizenship, ID and payment.
4. Have your photo taken there, check it, and hand in your application.

This saves a trip, but the photo can cost more than at a store, and appointment slots can fill up weeks ahead in busy seasons.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the best place to get a passport photo?

It depends on what you need. A store photo counter is quick for adults, a studio is better for babies and special cases, a post office lets you apply in the same trip, and an online tool avoids the trip altogether.

### Do post offices take passport photos?

Many post offices that accept passport applications also take photos, but not all. Check with the official locator or call ahead.

### Are passport expediting services worth it?

Only in specific cases. The State Department says a courier will not get your passport faster than applying at a passport agency. They mainly help if you have urgent travel and cannot get to an agency in person.

### Can a passport photo service guarantee my photo will be accepted?

No. Some offer a free retake if a photo is rejected, but only the passport authority decides whether a photo is accepted.
