## How we compared passport photo options

We compared each option on five things, using each company's own website and the State Department's photo rules (checked November 6, 2026):

| Criterion | What we looked at |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Acceptance** | Does the option check the photo, and what happens if it is rejected? |
| **Price** | The listed price for two 2 × 2 inch prints, where the company publishes one |
| **Speed** | Appointment needed or walk-in, and how long the photo takes |
| **Digital copy** | Whether you get a file for online renewal, and how |
| **Convenience** | How many locations, and whether you can do it without going anywhere |

No option can promise that the State Department will accept a photo. Only the passport office decides, so check any photo yourself before you send it. Our [overview of passport photos and how to get one](/tool/passport-photo) explains what every photo must show.

## Best passport photo options by need

### Best for speed

**A walk-in store photo counter.** Walgreens says no appointment is needed and prints are "typically available in a few minutes," and CVS, Staples and Walmart Photo Centers also take walk-ins. Phone first to check the counter is staffed. More fast options are in our guide to [getting a photo the same day](/tool/same-day-instant-passport-photos).

### Best for price

**Taking the photo yourself and printing a 4 × 6 sheet.** One print can hold more than one passport photo and usually costs far less than a store photo. Among stores, Walmart had the lowest price we found: $7.64 for its passport photos on November 6, 2026. Our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost) compares every price we could confirm.

### Best for babies

**A studio, or a white sheet at home.** A photographer used to babies can take time to get a usable photo. At home, you can lay a baby on a plain white sheet and try as many times as you need. Our [guide to baby and newborn photos](/tool/infant-newborn-passport-photo) explains both methods.

### Best digital option

**Walgreens in store, or an online tool at home.** Walgreens emails a free digital copy with its passport photos if you ask for it when you order. CVS charges extra for a digital copy at select stores; Staples, FedEx Office and Walmart offer a copy on a USB drive. An online tool gives you a digital file as soon as you upload your photo.

### Best acceptance guarantee

**Staples.** Its "Perfect Passport Guarantee" promises to retake your photos for free if they are not accepted. The service is non-refundable, so the guarantee gets you new photos, not your money back. Walgreens describes its photos as "guaranteed to be government compliant," but its policy page does not set out retake terms, so ask in store.

## Best passport photo apps and websites

An app or website is only as good as the photo you give it. For a US passport, the most important thing is what the tool does to your picture. The State Department says: "Do not change your photo using computer software, phone apps or filters," and it checks photos for editing, including changes made with artificial intelligence. Look for a tool that:

- **Crops and sizes without editing your face** or smoothing your skin
- **Leaves the background alone for a US passport**, so take the photo against a plain white or off-white wall
- **Shows you the result before you pay**
- **Gives both files**: a digital photo for online renewal and a print sheet
- **Covers the country you need**, since sizes differ (2 × 2 inches for the US, 35 × 45 mm for many others)

The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) works this way for US passports: it measures, crops and sizes the photo and leaves the background as you took it. For a brand-by-brand look at online services, see our [comparison of online passport photo websites](/tool/best-online-passport-photo-services).

## Best passport photo studios: what to look for

A good studio is worth it when you want someone else to get every detail right. Before you book, check that it:

- Knows the size and rules for your country and document, not only the US 2 × 2 inch photo
- Uses a plain white or off-white backdrop and even lighting, with no shadows
- Does not retouch or "beautify" passport photos
- Offers a digital file if you need one, and says what it costs
- Will retake the photo if it is rejected

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the best place to get a passport photo?

It depends on what you need. A walk-in store is fastest, taking the photo yourself is cheapest, a studio is best for babies, and a store with a free emailed copy, or an online tool, is best if you are renewing online.

### Which store has the cheapest passport photos?

Of the prices we could confirm on November 6, 2026, Walmart was lowest at $7.64. Prices change and can vary by store, so check before you go.

### Do passport photo apps work for a US passport?

They can, as long as the photo itself meets the rules and the app does not edit your face or replace the background. The State Department rejects photos changed with apps, filters or AI.

### Which passport photo service has a guarantee?

Staples offers a free retake if your photo is not accepted, but no refund. Other stores do not publish retake terms, so ask before you pay.
