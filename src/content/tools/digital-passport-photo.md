## What is a digital passport photo?

It is the same photo as a printed passport photo, saved as a file. You need one when an application is made online. For a US passport, that means **online renewal**, which is open to adults who meet the State Department's conditions, including:

- You are 25 or older
- Your current passport was valid for 10 years, and it expires within a year or expired less than five years ago
- Your name, sex and other personal details are not changing
- You are not travelling within six weeks, and you are in a US state or territory when you apply

If you apply in person or renew by mail, you need a **printed** photo instead. Other countries' online applications, such as the UK's, use digital photos too, each with its own file rules.

## Digital photo file requirements

These are the rules for a US online passport renewal, from the State Department's upload guidance:

| Rule | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Format** | JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF |
| **File size** | Between 54 KB and 10 MB |
| **Pixel dimensions** | No exact size stated |
| **Colour** | In colour, sharp and in focus |
| **Age** | Taken in the last six months |
| **Original** | Not a scan or photo of a printed photo; no filters or edits |

### Format

Most phones already save in one of the accepted formats: iPhones usually use HEIC, and most Android phones use JPG. You do not need to convert the file.

### Pixel dimensions

The online renewal guidance does not give an exact pixel size. Instead, frame the photo so your head is centred and fully visible, with your shoulders in the picture, and zoom in if you are far away; a photo zoomed out too far can cause background errors.

The 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixel size and 240 KB limit often seen online belong to US **visa** and Diversity Visa photos, not passport renewal.

### File size

Between 54 KB and 10 MB. A photo straight from a phone is usually inside this range. Problems come from compressing it: sending a photo by text message lowers the quality, and the State Department advises against it. Move the file by cable, AirDrop, email attachment or a cloud folder instead.

## How to get a digital passport photo or digital copy

### Online

Take the photo at home against a plain white or off-white wall, then crop it to size yourself or with an online tool. The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) gives you a digital file and a print sheet. For a US passport it only crops and sizes the photo, without changing your face or background, because the State Department rejects edited photos. ## Can you convert a printed passport photo to digital?

Not for a US online renewal. The State Department's upload rules say the file must not be a scan or a photo of a printed photo, so scanning or photographing prints you already have does not work. Instead:

- **Ask for the digital copy when you buy.** Walgreens emails a free digital copy with its passport photos. Other stores may charge extra or put the file on a USB drive, usually only if you ask at the time.
- **Take a new photo.** A photo taken at home against a plain white or off-white wall gives you the original file the upload needs.

Some other forms do accept a scan. Check the rules of the form you are filling in before you scan a print.

## Upload your digital photo for online renewal

1. **Start your online renewal** on the State Department's website and reach the photo step.
2. **Upload the original file** from your phone or computer. Do not crop it into a tiny file, add a border or edit it.
3. **Let the photo tool check it.** It checks basic requirements, tells you what to change if the photo is not acceptable, and lets you try again with a different photo.
4. **Submit your application.** A State Department employee reviews the photo again after it is received.
5. **Watch your email.** If there is a problem with the photo, the State Department sends a letter or email asking for a new one.

If you cannot get a photo that passes, or you do not meet the online renewal conditions, you can renew by mail with a printed photo. Our guide to [photos for renewals and new applications](/tool/passport-application-photo) explains each route.

## Digital vs printed photos

| | Digital photo | Printed photo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Used for** | Online renewal | In-person applications and renewal by mail |
| **Size rule** | File type and 54 KB to 10 MB | Exactly 2 × 2 inches, head 1 to 1 3/8 inches |
| **Checked by** | An automatic tool, then a person | The acceptance agent and the passport office |
| **Common failure** | Compressed, edited or zoomed out too far | Wrong size, printer dots, wrong paper |

You can make a print from a correctly cropped digital file, but not the other way round: a scan or photo of a printed photo is not accepted online. Our guide to [printing passport photos at home or in store](/tool/print-passport-photos) shows how to lay out a print sheet.

## Electronic passport photos and e-passports

Every US passport issued by domestic passport agencies since August 2007 is an electronic passport, or e-passport. A chip in the back cover stores the information on the data page, including a digital image of your passport photo. The State Department makes that image from the photo you submit, printed or digital, so you do not need a special "e-passport photo". Our [guide to biometric photo standards](/tool/biometric-passport-photo) explains what the chip photo is used for. A sharp, unedited photo that meets the normal rules is what it needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use a store photo for online renewal?

Only the digital copy, not a scan or a photo of the print. Ask for the digital copy when you order; some stores email it free, others charge extra or put it on a USB drive.

### What size should a digital passport photo be?

For US online renewal, the file must be JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF and between 54 KB and 10 MB. The State Department does not set an exact pixel size.

### Can I take my own digital passport photo?

Yes. Have someone take it several feet away against a plain white or off-white wall, in even light, and upload the original file without filters or edits.

### What happens if my digital photo is rejected?

The upload tool tells you what to change before you submit. If a problem is found later, the State Department sends a letter or email asking for a new photo.

### Is a digital photo the same for a visa?

No. US visa photos have their own digital rules, including a pixel size of 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 and a 240 KB limit. Check the rules for the document you are applying for.
