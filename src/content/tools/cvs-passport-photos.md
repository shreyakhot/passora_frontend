## Does CVS take passport photos?

Yes, but not at every store. CVS says passport photos "are available in select CVS Pharmacy locations," and they "must be taken in store." You cannot order CVS passport photos online or have them made from a photo you upload.

CVS says its stores use the KODAK Moments Passport & ID Photo System, which it says "automatically verifies that your photos meet all requirements for passport photos." That check helps, but it is not a guarantee of acceptance, so look at the print yourself before you leave (see the checklist below).

## CVS passport photo cost

CVS lists the price on its [passport photos page](https://www.cvs.com/photo/passport-photos). The standard order is two 2 × 2 inch prints. Prices change and can vary by location, so check the page or ask in store before you go.

Extras cost more:

- **Digital copy:** offered at select locations for an extra fee and sent by email.
- **USB drive:** CVS also lists a Kodak USB memory stick option.

To compare CVS with other stores and with doing it yourself, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## Finding a CVS that takes passport photos

### Use the store locator

The [CVS store locator](https://www.cvs.com/store-locator/landing) can find stores with passport photo labs. Each store's page lists its photo services, so check that **Passport photos** appears before you go.

### Walk-in, no appointment

CVS says "you do not need an appointment to take a Passport photo at CVS," and you do not need to bring anything. The photo counter must be staffed, though, so phone the store first if you are going early, late or at the weekend. Opening hours vary by store and the photo service may not run for every hour the pharmacy is open.

## How a CVS passport photo works

### In store

1. A staff member takes your photo against a plain background.
2. The photo system checks it and crops it to 2 × 2 inches.
3. You get two prints, usually within minutes.

### Digital copy

If you plan to renew your US passport online, you need a digital photo, not a print. Ask for the digital copy option before the photo is taken, as it is only offered at some stores. If your store does not offer it, see our guide to [getting a digital passport photo](/tool/digital-passport-photo).

## Visa, ID and other photos at CVS

CVS describes its service as passport, visa and ID photos. It says its ID photos "can be used for passports, visas, work IDs, and most other ID cards."

For visas, CVS notes that most embassies ask for 2 × 2 inch photos, but tells customers to "confirm the visa photo guidelines for the country you plan to visit." Many countries, including the UK and the Schengen countries, use 35 × 45 mm instead. If you need a size other than 2 × 2, ask the store before you pay.

Standard CVS photo prints, such as wallet prints, are not ID photos. Printing your own photo as a wallet print will not give you a correctly sized passport photo.

## Check your CVS photo before you leave

- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows behind you
- Face evenly lit, both eyes open, neutral expression, mouth closed
- No glasses; no hat unless it is religious or medical
- Head between 1 and 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm), chin to top of hair
- Sharp print, not blurred or grainy

If anything looks wrong, ask for a retake while you are there. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a CVS passport photo without an appointment?

Yes. CVS says no appointment is needed. Call ahead to make sure someone who can take photos is working when you plan to go.

### Can I upload my own photo to CVS to print as a passport photo?

No. CVS says passport photos must be taken in store. If you want to use your own photo, crop it to the right size yourself and print it as a standard photo print.

### Does CVS give you a digital passport photo for online renewal?

At select locations, for an extra fee, sent by email. Ask before your photo is taken.

### Can CVS take a photo for a visa?

CVS takes visa and ID photos, mostly in the 2 × 2 inch size. Check the size the country requires first; many use 35 × 45 mm.

### Does CVS accept passport applications?

CVS's passport service covers the photo only. To apply in person, use the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/) to find a post office or other acceptance facility.
