## Does Walmart take passport photos?

Yes, at stores with a Photo Center. Walmart says passport photos "are currently a Photo Center in-store service only" and that "Photo Center associates take your passport photo in store." You walk in, with no appointment needed.

Some stores have a self-service Photo Center kiosk with a built-in camera. Where that hardware has not been installed yet, a Photo Center associate takes the photo for you.

Walmart says your photo "is taken and reviewed in store to meet all government regulations." It does not describe this as a guarantee, so check the print yourself before you leave.

## Walmart passport photo cost

On **November 5, 2026**, Walmart's [passport photos page](https://photos3.walmart.com/category/83-passport-photos) listed "Passport Photos - Full Photo" at **$7.64**. Prices can change and may vary by store, so check the page or ask at the Photo Center.

An optional USB drive with your images, for online renewals, is also offered. Walmart does not show a price for it on that page, so ask in store.

To see how Walmart compares with pharmacies, studios and doing it yourself, read our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## Photo Center locations and hours

### Which stores have a Photo Center

Not every Walmart has a Photo Center. Walmart's passport page talks about walking into the Photo Center "at your local Walmart Supercenter." Smaller-format stores may not have one.

### Check before you go

1. Use the Store Finder on Walmart's site and open your local store's page to see whether it lists Photo Center services.
2. Phone the store to check the Photo Center is open and staffed at the time you plan to visit. Photo Center hours can be shorter than the store's opening hours.
3. If you need a USB copy, ask whether the store has one available.

## What you get: 2 × 2 inch prints

Walmart says "all photos are printed as 2×2 photo prints," which it describes as suitable for US passports, visas, green cards and other government-issued ID.

That is the right size for a US passport and many US documents. It is the wrong size for the UK, Schengen countries, India (35 × 45 mm) and Canada (50 × 70 mm). If you search for a passport size photo at Walmart for one of those countries, you will need another option. Our guide to [getting prints in other passport sizes](/tool/passport-size-photo-near-me) explains where to go.

For more on the US format, see our [2 × 2 inch photo guide](/tool/2x2-passport-photo).

## Ordering online vs in store

You cannot order a Walmart passport photo online. Walmart's photo site lists passport photos as a same-day, in-store service, with no upload, shipping or delivery option. Ordinary photo prints you order online are not sized or checked as passport photos.

Some "2x2 passport photo" listings on walmart.com are sold by marketplace sellers, not by Walmart's Photo Center. Read who the seller is before you order.

## Walmart does not accept passport applications

Walmart says it is not an acceptance facility for passport applications. If you are applying in person, take your photo with you to a post office or other facility listed on the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an appointment for a Walmart passport photo?

No. Walmart says you can walk into the Photo Center with no appointment. Call ahead to check the Photo Center is staffed when you plan to go.

### How long does a Walmart passport photo take?

Walmart sells it as a same-day service, and the prints are usually ready while you wait.

### Can Walmart print my own photo as a passport photo?

Walmart's passport photos are taken in store. Walmart's photo site does not offer a way to upload your own photo as a passport photo.

### Can I use a Walmart passport photo for online renewal?

You need a digital file, not a print. Ask for the optional USB copy, then upload the image when you renew. Check the State Department's online renewal instructions first.
