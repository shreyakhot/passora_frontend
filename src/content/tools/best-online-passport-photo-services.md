### Photo-Me (digital upload)

Photo-Me runs photo booths in the UK. Choose "Digital UK Passport" in a booth and it prints your photos with a **digital photo code** at the top. You type the code into the GOV.UK online passport application, which then fetches the photo.

- **Price:** "Digital UK passport photos cost £10.00."
- **Timing:** allow up to 72 hours for the code to work. It is valid for 30 days, then the digital photos are deleted.
- **What it covers:** UK passport applications on GOV.UK, with a separate option for Irish passports. Codes are not available for driving licences or visas.

## How to choose an online service

- **Add up the total.** Check the price of the digital file, the prints and the delivery before you upload, not just the headline price.
- **Read the refund terms.** Look for what proof you need, the deadline, and how the money comes back.
- **Check what it changes.** For a US passport, a service that only crops and sizes your photo is safer than one that replaces the background.
- **Ask who checks it.** An automatic check catches size problems; a person can also spot shadows, glare or a closed eye.
- **Check privacy.** Look for how long your photo is kept and whether it is used for anything else.
- **Check your document.** Make sure the service lists your country and document: a 2 × 2 inch photo for the US, a 35 × 45 mm photo for many other countries, or a code for an online application.

## Can you do passport photos online?

For a US passport, yes, if the photo meets every rule. The State Department accepts a photo taken by "a friend or family member." What it checks closely is editing: it says "Do not change your photo using computer software, phone apps or filters," and it looks for digital changes, including AI. Most of the services above can replace or adjust the background, so take your photo against a plain white or off-white wall in even light. Then little or nothing needs changing, whichever service you use.

Other countries differ. Canada requires a commercial photographer, and the UK's online application takes either a code from a booth or shop or a digital photo you take yourself. Our [guide to digital passport photos](/tool/digital-passport-photo) covers online applications in more detail.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ePassportPhoto legit?

It is a registered company, Biometric Photo International in Poland, and it publishes its price, terms and refund conditions on its website. As with any service, read the refund terms before you pay. No service can promise acceptance: only the passport office decides.

### How much does an online passport photo cost?

Of the services that list a price, a US digital passport photo cost $13.95 at both ePassportPhoto and PhotoAiD on November 16, 2026. Prints and delivery cost extra. Our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost) compares stores as well.

### What is a 200% refund guarantee?

ePassportPhoto and PhotoAiD both say they refund twice what you paid if the authority rejects your photo. Both ask for proof of the rejection, and PhotoAiD sets a 30-day deadline and pays the extra 100% by PayPal. Read the full terms before you rely on it.

### What is a Photo-Me digital photo code?

A code printed by a UK Photo-Me booth when you choose a digital UK passport photo. You enter it in the GOV.UK online passport application instead of uploading a file. It costs £10 and is valid for 30 days.

### Do online services edit my face?

The services above say they crop, resize and, in most cases, change the background. For a US passport, avoid any edit to your face or the background: the State Department refuses photos changed with software, apps, filters or AI.
