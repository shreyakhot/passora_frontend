## Does Rite Aid still offer passport photos?

No. Every Rite Aid store has closed, so there is nowhere to get a Rite Aid passport photo. When the last stores shut, Rite Aid's website said: "All Rite Aid stores have now closed."

Old map listings, coupon sites and review pages may still show Rite Aid photo prices or hours. They are out of date. If a store building near you still has Rite Aid signs, it is closed or now run by another business.

### Rite Aid store closures

- **May 2025:** Rite Aid filed for bankruptcy for the second time in under two years and began closing its remaining stores.
- **Late September 2025:** the last stores closed, ending the chain after 63 years. News reports named the final two as Bainbridge Island, Washington, and Bend, Oregon.
- **Prescriptions:** pharmacy records moved to other pharmacies. News reports said former customers could use Rite Aid's website to request their records or find another pharmacy.

## What happened to Rite Aid photo printing?

Rite Aid's in-store photo services, including passport photos and photo prints, ended when the stores closed. A Rite Aid photo counter cannot retake, reprint or replace a photo any more. To print a passport photo you already have, see our guide to [printing passport photos at home or in a store](/tool/print-passport-photos).

Any passport photo taken at Rite Aid is now too old for a US passport anyway: the State Department asks for a photo taken in the last 6 months.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are any Rite Aid stores still open?

No. Rite Aid closed its last stores in late September 2025.

### Are there Rite Aid passport photo hours or locations?

No. With every store closed, there are no Rite Aid passport photo hours or locations. Listings that still show them are out of date.

### How much did a Rite Aid passport photo cost?

The price no longer matters, because the service has ended. For current prices at other stores, see our guide to passport photo costs.

### Where is the closest place to get a passport photo instead of Rite Aid?

Usually a CVS, Walgreens or Walmart nearby, or a post office, FedEx Office or Staples. Phone first to check someone can take the photo.

### Can I still use a passport photo I got at Rite Aid?

Not for a US passport. Any Rite Aid photo is now more than 6 months old, which is older than the State Department allows.
