## Does Costco do passport photos?

No. Costco's photo centres were where its warehouses took passport photos, and Costco closed the photo department at all locations on **Sunday, February 14, 2021**. Costco Canada's notice lists passport photos among the services that ended, along with custom framing and ink cartridge refills.

Some older warehouse listings and third-party sites still show a photo centre or passport photos. They are out of date. Costco has not reopened in-warehouse photo services.

## Do you need a membership?

Not any more, because there is no service to use. A membership does not give access to passport photos at any warehouse.

Today the only Costco photo service is online. In the US, Costco's photo page now promotes **Shutterfly**, with a discount for Costco members, for prints and photo books delivered to your home. It is a print shop, not a passport photo service: it does not take your photo or check it against passport rules.

## Costco passport photo cost

There is no current Costco passport photo price, because the service no longer exists. Any price you find online dates from before 2021.

For what other stores charge today, with the date each price was checked, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost). For comparison, on **November 16, 2026**, Walmart's passport photos page listed a passport photo at **$7.64**, for pickup at a Walmart Photo Center.

## Costco Canada passport photos

Costco Canada closed its photo centres on the **same day, February 14, 2021**, and its notice says passport photos "will no longer be available". Members can still order prints and photo products online through the Costco Photo Centre website, for delivery.

For a Canadian passport, this matters more than in the US: the photo must be taken by a commercial photographer or studio, who writes the studio's name, address and the date on the back. Pharmacies and photo studios do this. Shoppers Drug Mart, for example, says its stores take passport photos without an appointment, as well as citizenship, PR card and visa photos. Our [guide to Canadian photo rules](/tool/canadian-passport-photos) covers the size (50 × 70 mm) and what to check before you pay.

## Finding a warehouse with photo service

You will not find one. No Costco warehouse in the US or Canada takes passport photos today. If a store locator or map listing still shows a photo centre, it is out of date.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Costco still take passport photos?

No. Costco closed the photo department in all its warehouses on February 14, 2021, and passport photos ended with it.

### Does Costco Canada do passport photos?

No. Costco Canada closed its photo centres on the same day, and its notice says passport photos are no longer available.

### Can I order passport photos from Costco online?

No. Costco's online photo service makes prints and photo products. It does not take passport photos or check them against government rules.

### Where is the closest alternative to Costco for passport photos?

In the US, Walmart, Walgreens, CVS and many post offices take passport photos. In Canada, pharmacies such as Shoppers Drug Mart and local photo studios do.
