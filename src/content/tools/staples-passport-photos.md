## Does Staples take passport photos?

Yes. Staples says passport photo services "are offered at every Staples location, no appointment needed." Its store pages say photos are usually ready in about 10 minutes.

Staples also says it takes "passport and visa photos for nearly every country." Ask in store for the specific photo you need, and bring the size if the country uses something other than the US 2 × 2 inch format.

## Staples passport photo options and price

Staples sells three passport photo options:

| Option | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Prints only** | Two 2 × 2 inch prints |
| **Digital file** | A digital copy of your photo on a USB drive |
| **Bundle** | Two 2 × 2 inch prints plus a digital file |

Staples lists the prices on its [passport photo services page](https://www.staples.com/stores/services/passport-photos). Prices change and can vary, so check the page or ask in store. If you pay online first, Staples asks you to bring your order confirmation email to the store.

The digital file is the one to choose if you plan to renew online, need reprints later or have other forms that ask for an upload.

To compare Staples with pharmacies, studios and doing it yourself, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## The Perfect Passport Guarantee

Staples offers what it calls a "Perfect Passport Guarantee": if your photo is not accepted the first time, "we'll retake them for free." Its product pages tie this to the US Department of State's requirements and describe the service as non-refundable. So the guarantee gets you a free retake, not your money back, and you still lose the time.

## Staples passport renewal: photo only or full service?

"Staples passport renewal" covers two different things.

**1. The photo.** Staples takes your photo. You then renew yourself: by mail, or online if you are eligible. Our guide to [photos for renewals and new applications](/tool/passport-application-photo) explains how each route works.

**2. Expedited passport services, run by a partner.** Staples stores list "Expedited Passports" among their travel services, alongside TSA PreCheck renewals and online notary. Staples' expedited passport service is "powered by HelloGov," a private company: you get your photo at Staples, then complete your application online with HelloGov. Its fees are on top of the government's passport fees.

A private expediting service is optional. The State Department itself offers expedited processing for an extra government fee, and in-person appointments at passport agencies for urgent travel. Compare those before you pay a private service.

If you need to apply in person, for a first passport or a child's passport, use the State Department's [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/) to find a post office or other acceptance facility near you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an appointment for a Staples passport photo?

No. Staples says no appointment is needed at any of its stores.

### Can Staples take a passport photo for another country?

Staples says it takes passport and visa photos for nearly every country. Tell the staff which country and document it is for, and check the size on the official website first.

### What happens if my Staples passport photo is rejected?

Under the Perfect Passport Guarantee, Staples retakes the photo for free. The service is non-refundable, so you get new photos, not a refund.

### Can I renew my passport at Staples?

Staples takes the photo. Its expedited passport service is run by HelloGov, a private company that charges its own fees. You can also renew directly with the State Department by mail or, if you are eligible, online.
