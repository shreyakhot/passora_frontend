## The most common passport photo sizes

Most countries use one of a handful of sizes. Pixel sizes below are for printing at 300 dpi, which is standard for photo prints.

| Size | Millimetres | Inches | Pixels at 300 dpi | Shape | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 × 2 in** | 51 × 51 | 2 × 2 | 600 × 600 | Square | US passports and visas |
| **35 × 45 mm** | 35 × 45 | 1.38 × 1.77 | 413 × 531 | Portrait (7:9) | UK, Schengen countries, India |
| **50 × 70 mm** | 50 × 70 | 1.97 × 2.76 | 591 × 827 | Portrait (5:7) | Canadian passports |
| **33 × 48 mm** | 33 × 48 | 1.30 × 1.89 | 390 × 567 | Portrait | Chinese passports and visas |

Each size has its own guide:

- The square US format: [2×2 inch photo rules and printing](/tool/2x2-passport-photo)
- The most widely used portrait format: [35 × 45 mm photo rules](/tool/35x45mm-passport-photo)
- Every other country: [photo sizes listed country by country](/tool/passport-photo-size-by-country)

Rules for the same country can differ between a passport and a visa, and between paper and online applications. Always check the issuing authority's current rules before you print.

## How big your head should be

The frame size is only half the rule. Each country also sets how much of the photo your head should fill, measured from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head or hair. For a US passport, the head must measure between **1 and 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm)**, which is roughly half to two-thirds of the photo's height.

![Diagram of a US passport photo: a 2 by 2 inch frame with the head measuring 25 to 35 mm from chin to top of hair](/images/guides/us-passport-photo-size-diagram.svg)

A head that is too small or too large is one of the most common reasons for rejection. It usually happens when a photo is cropped by eye or stretched to fit. Photo makers and studios measure this for you, but it is worth checking with a ruler on the print.

## Print size vs digital size

A printed passport photo is defined in inches or millimetres. A digital photo is defined in pixels, and the two are linked by resolution:

> pixels = millimetres ÷ 25.4 × dpi

At 300 dpi, 35 mm becomes 413 pixels and 45 mm becomes 531 pixels. That is why a 413 × 531 pixel file prints at exactly 35 × 45 mm.

Online application systems often have their own digital rules that have nothing to do with print size: a minimum and maximum pixel size, a file size limit, and a file type such as JPEG. Some ask for an uncropped photo and crop it themselves. Follow the instructions on the application website, not the print size. Our guide to [preparing a photo for online applications](/tool/digital-passport-photo) covers the common digital rules.

## How to check the size of a photo you already have

**For a print:**

1. Measure the width and height with a ruler. A US photo should be exactly 2 × 2 inches. A 35 × 45 mm photo should be 3.5 × 4.5 cm.
2. Measure your head from the bottom of your chin to the top of your hair, and compare it with your country's range.
3. Check that your face is centred and your head is straight.

**For a digital file:**

1. Check the pixel dimensions. On Windows, right-click the file and choose *Properties* then *Details*. On a Mac, open it in Preview and choose *Tools* then *Show Inspector*.
2. Check the shape. A US photo must be square (1:1). A 35 × 45 mm photo has a 7:9 ratio, so 413 × 531 or 700 × 900 pixels both work for printing.
3. Check the application site's file size limit and file type.

If the size is wrong, do not stretch the photo to fit. Stretching distorts your face. Crop it from a larger original, or make a new one.

## Printing passport size photos

Passport photos are usually printed several to a sheet on standard 6×4″ (10 × 15 cm) photo paper, then cut out. A 6×4″ sheet holds six 2 × 2 inch photos or up to eight 35 × 45 mm photos. Print on glossy or matte photo paper. Ordinary office paper and photocopies are not accepted for paper applications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the size of a passport size photo in cm?

It depends on the country. The most common international size is 3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm). A US passport photo is 5.1 × 5.1 cm (2 × 2 inches), and a Canadian passport photo is 5 × 7 cm.

### Is a passport size photo the same as 2×2?

Only for the United States and a few other countries. In most of Europe, the UK and India, a passport size photo is 35 × 45 mm, which is narrower and taller than 2 × 2 inches. Check the size for the country that issues your document.

### What size is a passport photo in pixels?

For printing at 300 dpi, a 2 × 2 inch photo is 600 × 600 pixels and a 35 × 45 mm photo is 413 × 531 pixels. Online applications often set their own pixel and file size limits, so check the application site's instructions.

### Can I resize a normal photo to passport size?

Yes, if the original is sharp, recent, taken straight on against a plain light background, and has enough space around your head to crop. Resize by cropping, never by stretching. The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) crops to the right size and head height for the country you choose.

### Does the size include a white border?

No. The size is the size of the photo itself, edge to edge. Any white border from printing should be trimmed off.
