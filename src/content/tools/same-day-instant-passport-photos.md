## Fastest ways to get a passport photo (ranked by speed)

| Option | Typical time | You get | Appointment? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Photo at home + online tool | Minutes, once the photo is taken | Digital file now; prints after a kiosk trip | No |
| Walk-in store photo counter | A few minutes at the counter | Two 2 × 2 inch prints; digital copy at some stores | Usually no |
| Photo booth or kiosk | A few minutes | Prints | No |
| Studio | Same day if a slot is free | Prints, often a digital file | Usually yes |

Travel time and queues decide the real winner. A store five minutes away with no queue can beat anything else; a busy counter on a Saturday can take longer than doing it yourself.

### Online tool

If you can stand in front of a plain white or off-white wall with someone to take the photo, an online tool such as the [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) is the fastest route to a digital file: it measures, crops and sizes the photo in seconds. You still need a print for a paper application, which means printing a 4 × 6 inch sheet at a photo kiosk or at home. Our guide to [setting up the photo at home](/tool/passport-photo-at-home) covers the wall, light and camera position.

### Photo booth or kiosk

A booth is quick, but check it before you pay. The State Department lists "low quality vending machine" photos among those that are not acceptable, and some booths do not offer the US 2 × 2 inch size. Choose one that prints on photo paper at the right size, and check the print against the rules below.

### Walk-in retail

Pharmacies, office stores and supercenters with a photo counter are the most common same-day option:

- [Walgreens](/tool/walgreens-passport-photos) says no appointment is needed and prints are "typically available in a few minutes."
- [CVS](/tool/cvs-passport-photos) and [Staples](/tool/staples-passport-photos) also take walk-ins.
- [Walmart](/tool/walmart-passport-photos) takes photos at stores with a Photo Center.

The counter has to be staffed, and photo hours can be shorter than store hours. Phone first if you are going early, late or at the weekend. For every kind of place near you, see our guide to [nearby places that take passport photos](/tool/passport-photos-near-me).

### Studio appointment

A studio is rarely the fastest, but many can fit you in the same day. It is the better choice for a baby or anyone who needs time and care. See our guide to [choosing a professional photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me).

## What "instant" means: prints vs digital files

"Instant" means different things depending on what you need:

- **A print** is what you staple to a paper application (Form DS-11 or DS-82). A store hands you prints on the spot; a home photo needs printing first.
- **A digital file** is what you upload when you renew online. Some stores email one or put it on a USB drive, and an online tool gives you one straight away.

If you are renewing online, a store print does not help unless you also get the digital copy. Our guide to [getting a digital photo for your application](/tool/digital-passport-photo) explains the file rules.

## Same-day checklist

Before you leave or start:

1. Know which document and country the photo is for. A US passport photo is 2 × 2 inches; many other countries use 35 × 45 mm.
2. Take off glasses. They are not allowed in US passport photos unless you have a medical reason and a signed note.
3. Wear everyday clothes, not a uniform or anything that looks like one, and nothing white that blends into the background.
4. Decide whether you need prints, a digital file or both, and say so at the counter.
5. Check the photo before you leave (see below), and ask for a retake if anything is wrong.

## Urgent travel: photo timing and expedited passports

If your trip is close, the photo takes minutes; the passport is what takes time. The State Department offers:

- **Urgent appointments at a passport agency** if you are travelling internationally within 14 calendar days, or within 28 days if you also need a foreign visa. Appointments are free, are needed for urgent service, and may not be available straight away. You can book online or call 877-487-2778.
- **A separate service for life-or-death emergencies**, for an immediate family member's serious illness, injury or death abroad.
- **Expedited service** by mail or at an acceptance facility, for an extra fee, when the trip is further off.

Bring the photo with you: an in-person application needs one, and an agency appointment is not the place to discover the head size is wrong. A paid courier can carry your paperwork, but it does not make the State Department process it any faster. Our guide to [the different kinds of passport photo and application services](/tool/passport-photo-services) compares them.

## Are instant passport photos accepted?

Yes, if they meet the same rules as any other photo. Speed does not change the requirements:

- 2 × 2 inches, with the head 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair
- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
- Taken in the last six months
- Sharp, in colour, on photo paper, with no visible printer dots
- Unedited: "Do not change your photo using computer software, phone apps or filters"

A quick photo that fails one of these slows you down far more than a slow one that passes. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list, and our [guide to what a passport photo has to show](/tool/passport-photo) explains why the rules are so strict.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I get a passport photo in a few minutes?

At a walk-in photo counter at a pharmacy, office store or supercenter. Walgreens, for example, says no appointment is needed and prints are usually ready in a few minutes. Phone first to check the counter is staffed.

### Can I get a digital passport photo the same day?

Yes. Some stores email a digital copy or put it on a USB drive at the counter, and an online tool gives you a file as soon as the photo is taken. Ask for the digital copy before the photo is taken.

### Does a same-day photo get my passport faster?

No. The photo only removes one delay. For a trip within 14 days (or 28 days with a visa), book an urgent appointment at a passport agency.

### Are photo booth pictures OK for a US passport?

Only if they meet every rule. The State Department rejects low-quality vending machine photos, so check the size, sharpness and background before you use one.
