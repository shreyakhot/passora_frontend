## Target passport photos

Target does not advertise a passport photo service of its own. What some stores do have is a **CVS Pharmacy inside the Target**. CVS store pages for some of these in-Target pharmacies list passport photos among their services.

To find out whether your Target can help:

1. Look up the CVS Pharmacy in that Target on the CVS store locator.
2. Check whether "passport photos" is listed under the store's services.
3. Phone the pharmacy before you go, since CVS offers passport photos at select stores only.

If it does, you get the CVS service: two 2 × 2 inch prints, no appointment, taken by a pharmacy team member. Our [guide to the CVS photo service](/tool/cvs-passport-photos) covers what you get and what to check.

## Does AAA take passport photos?

Yes, many AAA clubs take passport photos at their branches, often at a discount for members. Non-members can usually use the service too, at a higher price. Each regional club sets its own prices and services.

### AAA passport photo prices

On **November 23, 2026**, AAA Club Alliance listed these passport photo prices:

| Membership | Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| Premier | Free, also for dependents under the legal driving age |
| Plus | $8.99 |
| Classic | $11.99 |
| Non-member | $14.99 |

AAA Club Alliance says no appointment is needed and that a digital photo for online renewal is available at most of its locations. It also offers expedited passport and visa help through a partner company, RushMyPassport, at a member discount.

### AAA passport photo cost at other clubs

Other AAA clubs set their own prices, and they differ, especially for non-members. Before you go:

- Find your local club and branch on the AAA website by entering your ZIP code.
- Check the club's passport photo page or phone the branch for the current price.
- Ask whether your membership level includes free photos, and whether children are covered.

AAA branches take the photo. To apply for a passport, you still use an acceptance facility such as a post office, or renew by mail or online.

## Kodak and other photo providers

A **Kodak** passport photo comes from a store, not from Kodak itself: the Kodak Passport and ID Photo System is software and equipment that retailers install. Kodak says it checks the photo's size, quality, head position and expression before printing, and gives customers a verification certificate with the prints. CVS, for example, uses it. A Kodak check helps, but the passport agency still decides whether a photo is accepted.

Other places that take passport photos:

- **The UPS Store:** many locations; stores are independently owned and set their own prices.
- **[Staples](/tool/staples-passport-photos):** prints, a USB copy or both, with a free retake if the photo is rejected.
- **[FedEx Office](/tool/fedex-office-passport-photos):** no appointment, prints or a USB copy.
- **[Post offices](/tool/usps-post-office-passport-photos):** many offices with passport services, usually by appointment.
- **Photo studios:** better for babies, unusual country sizes or anyone who wants retakes until the photo is right. See our guide to [finding a professional passport photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me).

Two chains you may remember no longer offer passport photos: [Costco closed its photo centres in 2021](/tool/costco-passport-photos), and [every Rite Aid store has closed](/tool/rite-aid-passport-photos).

## How to check whether a store offers passport photos

Before you drive to any store:

1. **Search the store locator.** Most chains list "passport photos" as a store service. If the listing does not mention it, assume it does not offer it.
2. **Phone the store.** Ask whether someone who can take passport photos is working when you plan to go. Passport photos often depend on trained staff.
3. **Say what the photo is for.** A US passport needs a 2 × 2 inch photo; other countries and visas need other sizes.
4. **Ask about the format.** Do you get prints, a digital file or both, and at what price?
5. **Ask about retakes.** Will they retake the photo if it is rejected?

Then check the photo yourself before you leave: plain white or off-white background, no shadows, eyes open, neutral expression, no glasses. Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list. No store nearby? Take the photo at home against a plain white wall and crop it with the [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Target do passport photos?

Target does not advertise a passport photo service of its own. Some Target stores have a CVS Pharmacy inside, and some of those list passport photos. Check the CVS store page for that location or phone the pharmacy.

### Does AAA do passport photos for non-members?

Usually, at a higher price than members pay. AAA Club Alliance, for example, listed $14.99 for non-members on November 23, 2026. Check your local club.

### Are AAA passport photos free?

For some members. AAA Club Alliance lists free photos for Premier members and their dependents under the legal driving age. Other levels and clubs pay a fee.

### What is a Kodak passport photo?

A photo taken with the Kodak Passport and ID Photo System, which many retailers use. It checks the photo before printing, but the passport agency still decides whether to accept it.

### How do I know if a store near me takes passport photos?

Check the store locator for "passport photos" under services, then phone to make sure someone trained is working when you plan to go.
