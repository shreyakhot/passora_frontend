## Do you need a new picture to renew your passport?

Yes. Every US passport application, including a renewal, needs **one colour photo taken in the last six months**. You cannot reuse the photo from your current passport, a copy of it, or a scan of an old print. For online renewal, a photo of a printed photo or a scan is not accepted either.

The photo must show how you look now. If your appearance has changed a lot, that is fine: the new photo is what matters.

## Renewal vs new application photo rules

The photo rules are the same for every route. The difference is the form and how the photo reaches the State Department:

| Route | Form | Photo | How you hand it in |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Renew by mail** | DS-82 | One 2 × 2 inch print | Stapled to the form |
| **Renew online** | Online application | Digital file | Uploaded during the application |
| **New passport in person** | DS-11 | One 2 × 2 inch print | Handed to the acceptance agent, not attached |

### Can you renew by mail?

You can usually renew by mail if your most recent passport:

- Was issued in the last 15 years, when you were 16 or older
- Is not damaged beyond normal wear, and was never reported lost or stolen
- Is in your current name, or you can document a name change with a certified copy of the legal document
- Can be sent in with your application

If not, you apply in person with Form DS-11, as for a first passport. Online renewal has stricter conditions, including being 25 or older; our guide to [preparing a digital photo for your application](/tool/digital-passport-photo) lists them.

## Photo size for application forms

All US passport forms use the same photo: **2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)**, with the head 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair, against a plain white or off-white background. It must be in colour, sharp, printed on matte or glossy photo paper and unedited. Our [guide to 2 × 2 inch photos](/tool/2x2-passport-photo) covers the size in pixels and centimetres, and the [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the rest.

## How to attach the photo to your application

### Renewal by mail (DS-82)

1. Check the photo against the rules before you attach it.
2. Staple it in the photo box on the form with **four staples, placed vertically in the corners**, as close to the outer edges of the photo as you can.
3. Do not bend the photo.
4. Print the form single-sided, then sign and date it.
5. Send it with your most recent passport and the fee by a trackable mail service. The mailing address is a PO box, so the State Department says not to use UPS, FedEx or DHL.

### New application in person (DS-11)

Bring the photo to your appointment, but **do not attach or staple it to the form**. The acceptance agent checks the photo and attaches it for you. Do not sign the DS-11 until the agent asks you to.

Most post offices that accept passport applications can also take the photo there; see our guide to [photos and applications at the post office](/tool/usps-post-office-passport-photos).

## Acceptable ID for a new passport

When you apply in person with Form DS-11, you show an ID and hand in a photocopy of it:

- **A physical, government-issued photo ID.** Examples include a valid driver's license or enhanced driver's license, a previous US passport, a naturalization certificate, and a government or military ID.
- **No digital IDs.** Mobile driver's licenses and other digital IDs are not accepted.
- **A photocopy of the front and back** of each ID you present, on plain white 8.5 × 11 inch paper, single-sided, not reduced in size.

You also need proof of US citizenship, such as a birth certificate, with its own photocopy. The State Department's photo ID page lists every accepted document and what to do if your ID is from another state. A renewal by mail uses your most recent passport instead, which you send in with the form.

## How to upload your photo for online renewal

For online renewal, you upload a digital photo instead of mailing a print:

- JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or HEIF, between 54 KB and 10 MB
- Taken in the last six months, in colour, sharp and unedited
- Not a scan or a photo of a printed photo, and not sent to yourself by text message

The upload tool checks the photo and tells you what to change before you submit. A State Department employee reviews it again later, and asks for a new photo by letter or email if there is a problem. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use my old passport photo to renew?

No. The photo must be taken in the last six months, and scans or copies of old photos are not accepted.

### How many photos do I need to renew my passport?

One. Renewal by mail needs one 2 × 2 inch print stapled to Form DS-82; online renewal needs one digital file.

### Do I staple the photo to Form DS-11?

No. For a new application in person, bring the photo and give it to the acceptance agent, who attaches it. Staple the photo only to Form DS-82 for a renewal by mail.

### Is the renewal photo size different from a new passport photo?

No. Every US passport photo is 2 × 2 inches with the head 1 to 1 3/8 inches tall. Only online renewal is different, because it uses a digital file instead of a print.
