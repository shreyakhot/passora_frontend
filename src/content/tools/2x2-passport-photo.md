## What is a 2x2 photo?

A 2x2 photo is a square photo, 2 inches wide and 2 inches high, showing the head and the top of the shoulders. "2x2", "2 by 2" and "2 × 2" all mean the same size.

### 2 × 2 in = 51 × 51 mm = 5.1 × 5.1 cm

Two inches is 50.8 mm, which official guidance rounds to **51 mm**. In centimetres that is about 5.1 × 5.1 cm. A 5 × 5 cm photo is close, but nearly a millimetre short on each side, so measure carefully if a form says "exactly" 2 × 2 inches.

## Documents that use 2x2 photos

| Document | Photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| US passport book and passport card | One 2 × 2 inch print, or a digital file for online renewal |
| US visas (applied for online) | A digital square photo uploaded with the application |
| USCIS forms, such as the green card application (Form I-485) | Usually two identical 2 × 2 inch passport-style prints |
| Some other countries' visas and ID documents | Check the issuing authority; many use 35 × 45 mm instead |

Driver's licences, state ID cards and trusted traveller cards are usually photographed at the office instead; our [guide to ID photos for licences, cards and badges](/tool/id-photo) explains which documents need a photo from you.

USCIS form instructions set their own details. Form I-485, for example, asks for two identical colour photos on thin glossy paper, unmounted and unretouched, and several USCIS form instructions ask you to write your name and A-Number lightly in pencil or felt pen on the back. Always follow the instructions for the form you are filing.

## 2x2 size in pixels, cm and mm

| Unit | 2 × 2 inch photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Inches | 2 × 2 |
| Millimetres | 51 × 51 |
| Centimetres | 5.1 × 5.1 |
| Pixels at 300 dpi (for printing) | 600 × 600 |
| Pixels at 600 dpi | 1200 × 1200 |
| US visa digital photo | 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels, square |

A file of 600 × 600 pixels prints at exactly 2 × 2 inches at 300 dpi, the usual resolution for photo prints. For US passport online renewal, the State Department sets file types and a file size range (54 KB to 10 MB) rather than an exact pixel size; our guide to [getting a digital photo for your application](/tool/digital-passport-photo) has the details.

## Head position in a 2x2 frame

The size of the square is only half the rule. Inside it:

- **Head height:** 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from the bottom of the chin to the top of the head, including hair. At 300 dpi that is 300 to 413 pixels.
- **In a digital visa photo:** the head should be 50% to 69% of the image's height.
- **Centred:** head and shoulders centred, facing the camera, not tilted.
- **Space around:** a little background above the head and on both sides.

![Diagram of a US passport photo: a 2 by 2 inch frame with the head measuring 25 to 35 mm from chin to top of hair](/images/guides/us-passport-photo-size-diagram.svg)

A photo that is the right size but has the head too small or too large is rejected just the same. This usually happens when a photo is resized to 2 × 2 instead of cropped, or when it is cropped by eye. The full list of what else the photo must meet is in our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements).

## Make a 2x2 photo online

You can also take the photo yourself and crop it to 2 × 2 inches:

1. Take the photo against a plain white or off-white wall, in even light, with someone else holding the camera. Our [tips on light, pose and clothing](/tool/how-to-take-a-good-passport-photo) help it look its best.
2. Crop it to a square, so the head measures 1 to 1 3/8 inches in the finished 2-inch photo.
3. Save it at 600 × 600 pixels or more, and print it on a 4 × 6 inch sheet on photo paper.

The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) does the cropping and measuring for you. For a US passport it only crops and sizes the photo and leaves your background as you took it, because the State Department rejects photos changed with software or AI. To print it, see our guide to [printing passport photos at home or in store](/tool/print-passport-photos).

## 2x2 vs 35 × 45 mm

| | 2 × 2 inch | 35 × 45 mm |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shape | Square | Portrait (taller than wide) |
| Size in inches | 2 × 2 | About 1.38 × 1.77 |
| Pixels at 300 dpi | 600 × 600 | 413 × 531 |
| Used by | US passports, visas and USCIS forms | UK, Ireland, Germany and other Schengen countries, India and many more |

The two are not interchangeable: cutting a 2 × 2 photo down to 35 × 45 mm makes the head the wrong size. See our [guide to the 35 × 45 mm format](/tool/35x45mm-passport-photo) for the countries that use it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is 2x2 in cm?

About 5.1 × 5.1 cm (51 × 51 mm). Two inches is exactly 5.08 cm.

### How many pixels is a 2x2 photo?

600 × 600 pixels at 300 dpi, the usual print resolution. US visa digital photos must be between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels.

### Can I use a 2x2 photo for a visa to another country?

Only if that country asks for 2 × 2 inches. Many countries use 35 × 45 mm or another size, so check the embassy's or consulate's photo rules.

### Is a 2x2 photo the same as a passport size photo?

In the US, yes. Elsewhere, "passport size" often means 35 × 45 mm. Our guide to [passport photo sizes around the world](/tool/passport-photo-size) explains the differences.
