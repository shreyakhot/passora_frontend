## Does the post office take passport photos?

Many do. The USPS says post offices that offer passport services can usually take your photo too: "Most of those locations can also take your passport photo." A post office that does not handle passports will not take one.

The photo is a service for passport applicants. It is easiest when you are applying in person at the same post office, but you can also use it for a renewal by mail or online.

## USPS passport photo cost

On **November 20, 2026**, the USPS listed:

| Fee | Amount | Paid to |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Post office photo fee (if needed) | **$15.00** | USPS |
| Post office acceptance fee (new applications in person) | **$35.00** | USPS |
| Passport application fee | Set by the State Department | U.S. Department of State |

If you bring your own photo, you pay no photo fee. The acceptance fee applies when the post office accepts a Form DS-11 application; it does not apply to a renewal you mail yourself. To compare the photo fee with stores, see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost).

## Which post offices offer photo service

To find one near you:

- **USPS locator:** search for post offices that offer passport services, then check whether the location lists photo service.
- **State Department locator:** the [Passport Acceptance Facility Locator](https://iafdb.travel.state.gov/) lists post offices and other places that accept applications.
- **Appointment scheduler:** the USPS appointment scheduler lets you choose the service and search by location, so it also shows you where to go.

Phone the post office if the listing is unclear, especially if you only want a photo.

## Photo service vs passport application acceptance

These are two different services that many post offices offer together:

| | Photo service | Application acceptance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| What it is | A clerk takes your 2 × 2 inch passport photo | A clerk checks your DS-11 application, ID and documents, and sends them to the State Department |
| Who needs it | Anyone without a photo | First-time applicants, children under 16, and anyone who cannot renew by mail or online |
| Fee | $15.00 | $35.00, plus the State Department's fees |

If you are applying in person, bring your photo or book the photo with your appointment. Do not staple the photo to a DS-11; the clerk does that. For each route and how to attach the photo, see our guide to [photos for renewals and new applications](/tool/passport-application-photo).

## Appointments and hours

The USPS asks you to **book an appointment** for passport services, including photos:

- **Online:** use the Retail Customer Appointment Scheduler, choose the service, then search by location or date.
- **In the lobby:** use a self-service kiosk and choose *Other Services*, then *Passport Scheduler*.
- **Walk-ins:** some post offices offer limited walk-in hours. Check the location before you go.

To change or cancel, use *Manage Appointments* in the scheduler with your confirmation number. Passport hours can be shorter than the post office's normal opening hours, and appointments can book up, so book early if your trip is close.

## Digital copies and other USPS photo options

The USPS says many post offices offer both **printed** and **digital** photos:

- **Printed photos** are for new applications and renewals by mail.
- **Digital photos**, provided on a USB drive, are for online renewal. They must meet the State Department's photo guidelines.

Ask for the digital copy when you book, as not every post office offers it. Our guide to [getting a digital photo for your application](/tool/digital-passport-photo) explains the file rules. If you need a photo for another country's passport or visa, ask the post office first whether it can take one.

## Check your post office photo before you leave

- 2 × 2 inches, head 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair
- Plain white or off-white background, no shadows
- Eyes open, neutral expression, no glasses
- Sharp, with no blur or grain

Our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements) has the full list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does the post office charge for passport photos?

The USPS listed a $15.00 photo fee on November 20, 2026. If the post office also accepts your application, the $35.00 acceptance fee is separate.

### Do I need an appointment for a passport photo at the post office?

Usually. The USPS asks you to book passport services online or at a lobby kiosk, and some post offices offer limited walk-in hours.

### Can the post office give me a digital passport photo?

Many post offices offer digital photos on a USB drive, for online renewal. Ask when you book.

### Can I get just a photo at the post office without applying?

Often yes, at post offices with photo service. Phone first or book the photo service in the scheduler.

### What are the passport fees at the post office?

If a post office accepts your passport application, it charges a $35.00 acceptance fee, plus $15.00 if it also takes your photo (USPS, November 20, 2026). The passport application fee itself is paid separately to the U.S. Department of State.
