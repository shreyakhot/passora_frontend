## What is 35 × 45 mm?

It is a portrait-shaped photo, **35 mm wide and 45 mm high**. Some forms write it the other way round, as 45 × 35 mm (height first); it is the same photo. The proportions are 7:9, so it is taller than it is wide.

### Conversion to inches, cm and pixels

| Unit | 35 × 45 mm photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Millimetres | 35 × 45 |
| Centimetres | 3.5 × 4.5 |
| Inches | About 1.38 × 1.77 |
| Pixels at 300 dpi (for printing) | 413 × 531 |
| Pixels at 600 dpi | 827 × 1063 |

Pixels for printing follow one formula: pixels = millimetres ÷ 25.4 × dpi. Online applications usually set their own minimum pixel size, which is often larger. The UK's online passport application, for example, asks for a digital photo at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall.

## Countries that use 35 × 45 mm

| Country or document | Size | Head or face size | Background |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| UK passport | 35 × 45 mm | 29–34 mm, chin to crown | Plain cream or light grey |
| Germany and other Schengen visas | 35 × 45 mm | 32–36 mm (face fills 70–80% of the photo) | Plain and light; see the embassy |
| Ireland passport | 35 × 45 mm minimum, 38 × 50 mm maximum | Head to mid-torso, with space around the head | Plain light grey, white or cream |
| India passport | 3.5 × 4.5 cm | Full face, centred | Plain white |
| Australia passport | 35–40 mm wide × 45–50 mm high | 32–36 mm, chin to crown | Plain white or light grey |

Many other countries and visas use the same frame, but not always with the same head size, background or paper. Australia, for example, asks for prints on glossy paper of at least 200 gsm, not inkjet. Check the issuing authority's rules, or our [list of photo sizes for every country](/tool/passport-photo-size-by-country).

## Head and face size inside the frame

The head size is where 35 × 45 mm photos most often go wrong. A UK photo with a 36 mm head is fine for a German visa and too big for a UK passport.

- Measure from the **bottom of the chin to the crown** (the top of the head), as the UK and Australia do. The UK measures to the top of the head, not the top of the hair.
- Leave space above the head and on both sides.
- Keep the head straight and centred, facing the camera.

German guidance gives a safe target of 32–36 mm for adults and a wider range of 22–36 mm for children aged 9 and under. For a Schengen visa, our guide to the [photo size for a Schengen visa](/tool/schengen-eu-photo-size) goes into more detail. The other rules, such as background, expression and glasses, are in our [passport photo rules checklist](/tool/passport-photo-requirements).

## 35 × 45 mm vs 2 × 2 inch

| | 35 × 45 mm | 2 × 2 inch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shape | Portrait | Square |
| Size in mm | 35 × 45 | 51 × 51 |
| Pixels at 300 dpi | 413 × 531 | 600 × 600 |
| Head size (examples) | UK 29–34 mm; Schengen 32–36 mm | US 25–35 mm |

You cannot trim a 2 × 2 inch photo down to 35 × 45 mm: the head ends up the wrong size and too close to the edges. Make each size from the original photo. For the US square format, see our [guide to 2 × 2 inch photos](/tool/2x2-passport-photo).

## Make a 35 × 45 photo online

1. Take a photo against a plain, light wall in even light, with someone else holding the camera. Check whether your country wants white, cream or light grey. Our [advice on looking your best within the rules](/tool/how-to-take-a-good-passport-photo) covers light, pose and clothing.
2. Crop it to 7:9 so the head is the size your country asks for.
3. Save it at 413 × 531 pixels or more for printing, or at the size the online application asks for.
4. Print it on photo paper. With cutting margins, six 35 × 45 mm photos fit on one 6 × 4 inch sheet.

The [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) crops to the frame and head size for the country you choose and gives you a print sheet and a digital file. Some countries do not accept photos you take yourself (Canada, for example, requires a commercial photographer), and the UK says photos from a booth or shop are more likely to be approved than one taken with your own device, so check before you start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is 35 × 45 mm in cm?

3.5 × 4.5 cm: 3.5 cm wide and 4.5 cm high.

### What is 35 × 45 mm in pixels?

413 × 531 pixels at 300 dpi, the usual print resolution. Online applications often ask for more; the UK, for example, asks for at least 600 × 750 pixels.

### Is 45 × 35 mm the same as 35 × 45 mm?

Yes. Both describe a photo 35 mm wide and 45 mm high; some forms list the height first.

### Is a UK passport photo 35 × 45 mm?

Yes. GOV.UK asks for printed photos 45 mm high and 35 mm wide, with the head 29–34 mm from chin to crown, against a plain cream or light grey background.

### Can I use a 35 × 45 mm photo for a US passport?

No. A US passport photo must be 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm).
