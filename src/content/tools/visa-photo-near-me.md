## What to ask for by destination

The person taking the photo needs to know three things: **the country, the document** (visa, passport or residence card) and **the format** (prints, a digital file or both).

### US visa

- Ask for a **2 × 2 inch** photo on a **white** background, with **no glasses**. Our [guide to the 2 × 2 inch format](/tool/2x2-passport-photo) explains the size.
- Ask for a **digital copy** too. Most applicants upload the photo with the DS-160 form; it must be square, 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels, JPEG and no more than 240 kB.
- Bring the printed photo to your interview if the upload failed.

Our [guide to visa photo rules](/tool/visa-photo) has the full US list.

### Schengen

- Ask for **35 × 45 mm** prints with the face filling 70–80% of the photo (32–36 mm from chin to crown). Our [guide to the 35 × 45 mm format](/tool/35x45mm-passport-photo) has the conversions.
- Check the background colour with the consulate or visa centre: most ask for white or light-coloured.
- Many visa centres take your photo when you give your fingerprints, so check before paying for prints.

See the [photo size for a Schengen visa](/tool/schengen-eu-photo-size) for each step.

### Other countries

- **India:** the e-Visa needs only a square digital file. Paper applications through the visa centre have their own size; see our [guide to Indian visa and OCI photos](/tool/indian-passport-photo-oci-photo).
- **Canada:** a visitor visa photo must be at least 35 × 45 mm. Canadian passports and citizenship need a 50 × 70 mm photo with the studio's details on the back; see our [guide to Canadian photo rules](/tool/canadian-passport-photos).
- **Anywhere else:** bring the embassy's photo rules on your phone. Our [list of photo sizes for every country](/tool/passport-photo-size-by-country) is a starting point.

## Visa photo studios and photographers

A local photo studio is the better choice when:

- The country needs an unusual size or background that a store kiosk does not offer.
- The rules require a professional photographer, as Canada does for passports and citizenship.
- You need several documents at once, such as photos for a visa, a residence permit and a work permit.
- You are photographing a baby or someone who cannot stand.

Many studios advertise "passport and visa photos" and handle any country. Phone ahead, name the country and size, and ask whether they can provide a digital file. Our guide to [finding a professional passport photographer](/tool/passport-photographer-near-me) covers what to ask and what it costs.

## Same-day visa photos

Store photos are usually ready in minutes, so you can get a visa photo on the day you need it. To avoid a second trip:

- Phone before you go to confirm someone is there to take photos, especially late in the day.
- Check the photo before you leave: size, background, head size and no glasses for a US visa.
- If you need a digital file, make sure you have it (by email, link or USB) before you go.

For more on getting photos fast, see our guide to [getting photos the same day](/tool/same-day-instant-passport-photos).

## Online alternative

If no nearby store can make the size you need, take the photo at home against a plain light wall and use the [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker). Choose your destination, crop the photo to its size and head size, then upload the file or print the sheet at a photo counter. Check first that your destination accepts photos you take yourself; Canada, for example, does not for passports and citizenship.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Walgreens take a photo for a foreign visa?

Walgreens lists a number of countries it can make photos for, including Canada, China, India, Mexico, the UK and several Schengen countries, and says other countries may be available in store. Ask your local store before you go.

### Do I need an appointment for a visa photo?

Not at Walgreens, Staples or FedEx Office, which say no appointment is needed. Studios often work by appointment, so phone ahead.

### Can I get a digital visa photo at a store?

Walgreens emails a digital copy, and Staples and FedEx Office offer one on a USB drive. Make sure the file meets your destination's upload rules.

### Is a passport photo from a store accepted for a US visa?

Only if it meets the visa rules: taken in the last 6 months, no glasses, white or off-white background. Tell the store it is for a US visa.
