## Print options compared

| Option | Good for | Watch out for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Photo kiosk or store photo counter** | Cheapest prints on real photo paper, ready in minutes | The kiosk may crop or "fit" the image; choose no borders and no auto-crop |
| **Order prints online for pickup** | Planning ahead | Same scaling risk; check the preview before you pay |
| **Home inkjet printer** | Instant reprints | Needs photo paper and the right settings; cheap inks can show printer dots |
| **Compact 4×6 photo printer** | Frequent ID photos, families | Printer and paper cost; many print only 4×6 |
| **Have the photo taken in store** | No setup at all | Costs more than printing a sheet; see our [guide to what passport photos cost](/tool/passport-photo-cost) |

Printing a sheet of photos yourself is usually the cheapest route, because one 4×6 inch print can hold several passport photos.

## The official print rules (US passport)

The State Department asks for a print that is:

- On **matte or glossy photo-quality paper**
- Sharp, not "too blurry, grainy, pixelated or printed on the wrong paper"
- Free of **visible printer dots**
- An original print, not a photocopy or a scan of another photo
- In colour, at **2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)**, with the head 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of hair
- Unedited: "Do not change your photo using computer software, phone apps or filters."

Ordinary office paper, home laminating and printing on the back of a photo do not meet these rules. The full list is in our [checklist of passport photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements).

## How to print passport photos on a 4×6 sheet

1. **Start with a correctly sized photo.** Crop it so the head fills the right part of the frame. A photo that is only resized, not cropped, will have the wrong head size.
2. **Lay it out on a 6 × 4 inch canvas at 300 dpi.** That is 1800 × 1200 pixels. Each 2 × 2 inch photo is 600 × 600 pixels. The free [passport photo sheet maker](/passport-photo-print-template-generator) does this for you.
3. **Leave space to cut.** A layout with a margin and small gaps fits two US 2 × 2 inch photos on one 6 × 4 sheet, or six 35 × 45 mm photos. Six 2 × 2 photos only fit edge to edge with no margin, and kiosks often trim the edges.
4. **Save as a high-quality JPEG** at the full 1800 × 1200 pixels. Do not send a screenshot.
5. **Print at 100% scale.** At home, choose the 4 × 6 in (10 × 15 cm) paper size, "actual size" and borderless off. At a kiosk, turn off auto-crop, auto-enhance and "fit to print".
6. **Cut and measure.** Cut along the guides and check one photo with a ruler: exactly 2 × 2 inches, with the head between 1 and 1 3/8 inches.

If the photo still needs cropping, the [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) crops it to your country's size and gives you a print-ready 6 × 4 sheet with the digital file.

## Print at home

### Paper

Use glossy or matte **photo paper** in the 4 × 6 inch size. Plain paper soaks up ink and looks grainy. Avoid textured papers such as "satin linen", which can show a pattern.

### Printer settings

- **Paper type:** set it to the photo paper you loaded, so the printer uses the right amount of ink
- **Quality:** best or high quality
- **Scale:** 100%, actual size, no "fit to page"
- **Colour:** colour on, no black-and-white or sepia
- **Enhancements:** off (no auto-fix, red-eye or sharpening)

Let the print dry before cutting it or stacking it.

## Print in store

### Sheet vs individual prints

Most photo kiosks sell 4 × 6 prints. Upload your sheet as a normal 4 × 6 print rather than as a "passport photo" product, unless the store offers a product made for your own photo. Store passport photo services, such as [CVS photo counters](/tool/cvs-passport-photos) and [Walmart Photo Centers](/tool/walmart-passport-photos), take a new photo in store and usually do not print your own file as a passport photo.

### Cost per print

A single 4 × 6 print is usually one of the cheapest items at a photo counter, so printing your own sheet costs far less than having photos taken in store. Prices vary by store, so check the kiosk before you print.

## Passport size photo printers

If you print ID photos often, a compact 4 × 6 photo printer is an option. Most use dye-sublimation, which gives smooth tones with no printer dots. Before buying, check that the printer can print at 100% scale with borders, and that paper is easy to find. For an occasional passport photo, a kiosk print is cheaper.

## Check the print before you send it

- Measure it: 2 × 2 inches, head 1 to 1 3/8 inches
- Look closely for dots, banding or streaks
- Check the colours look natural and the background is white or off-white, not grey or yellow
- Make sure the print is flat, clean and undamaged, with no fingerprints

If any of these fail, reprint rather than sending a doubtful photo.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I print passport photos at home?

Yes, for a US passport, as long as the print is on photo-quality paper, sharp, the right size and free of visible printer dots. Use photo paper and print at 100% scale.

### How many passport photos fit on a 4×6 print?

With a margin and room to cut, two US 2 × 2 inch photos or six 35 × 45 mm photos. Six 2 × 2 inch photos only fit with no margin at all, which kiosks may trim.

### Why did my passport photo print the wrong size?

The printer or kiosk scaled the image. Turn off "fit to page", auto-crop and borderless printing, and choose actual size (100%).

### Can I print my passport photo in black and white?

Not for a US passport. The State Department asks for colour photos.

### Do I need prints if I renew my passport online?

No. Online renewal uses a digital photo file instead of a print. Our guide to [preparing a digital photo for your application](/tool/digital-passport-photo) explains what to upload.
