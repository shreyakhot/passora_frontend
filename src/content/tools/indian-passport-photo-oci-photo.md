## Indian passport photo specs

| Rule | Indian passport photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| Size | 3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm), portrait |
| Background | Plain white |
| Face | Full face, front view, centred; about 80–85% of the photo from top of hair to chin |
| Colour | Colour |
| Glasses | Take them off |
| Age | Taken in the last 6 months |

When you need one depends on where you apply:

- **In India (Passport Seva Kendra, Post Office PSK):** you do not bring a photo. Passport Seva says a photograph is "not required" for applications submitted at a PSK or POPSK; staff take your photo and fingerprints at the counter.
- **Children under 4:** bring a 4.5 × 3.5 cm colour photo on a white background. Passport Seva says computer printouts are not accepted, so get a proper photo print.
- **Abroad (through an embassy, consulate or its service provider):** you upload a photo online and bring a print. In the US, the current VFS Global checklist for adult re-issue asks for a **35 × 45 mm** photo (630 × 810 pixels) on a **white** background, compliant with ICAO standards, and says the uploaded and printed photos must be identical. Older checklists asked for 2 × 2 inch photos, so download the current checklist for your service before printing.

Wear a coloured top so you stand out from the white background, and avoid a pure white shirt.

## Photo for Indian visa

The photo depends on how you apply.

| Visa route | Photo | Upload limits | Background |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| e-Visa (online only) | Digital only | JPEG, 10 KB to 1 MB, height and width equal | Plain light-coloured or white |
| Regular visa, online form | Digital upload | JPEG, 10 KB to 300 KB, square, 350 × 350 to 1000 × 1000 pixels | Plain light-coloured or white |
| Regular visa, printed photo (US, via VFS) | 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), glossy paper | Same as the online form | White |

For every visa photo: full face, front view, eyes open, **no glasses**, no shadows and no border. The VFS checklist in the US says to glue the printed photo to the form, not staple it, and that the printed and uploaded photos must be identical. For how Indian visa photos compare with other destinations, see our [guide to visa photo rules](/tool/visa-photo).

## OCI photo requirements

An OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) application asks you to upload a photo and your signature.

### Size

- **Square**, at least 51 × 51 mm, with the face covering about **80%** of the photo
- Front view of the head and shoulders, with the full face in the middle
- **Plain light-coloured background, not white**, with no border

The background is the main trap: an Indian passport photo is on white, but the OCI portal asks for a light colour that is not white.

### Upload size

| | Photo | Signature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Format | JPEG / JPG | JPEG / JPG |
| File size | 200 KB maximum | 200 KB maximum |
| Pixels | 200 × 200 minimum, 900 × 900 maximum, height and width equal | Not stated |

The portal rejects photos that do not meet these rules, which delays the application. If your service provider also asks for printed photos, follow its checklist for the size and number.

## "PP size" photos explained

**PP size** is short for *passport size*, a common term in India and across South Asia. A PP size photo usually means a **3.5 × 4.5 cm** photo, the size of an Indian passport photo. You may also hear it as *passport ke photo* ("passport photos" in Hindi).

Schools, banks, employers and government offices often ask for PP size photos for their forms. They are usually less strict than the passport office, but the safe default is 3.5 × 4.5 cm, plain white background, face centred. If a form asks for "stamp size" or gives a different size, follow the form. For other countries that use the same frame, see our [guide to the 35 × 45 mm format](/tool/35x45mm-passport-photo).

## Where to get Indian passport photos

- **In India:** any photo studio will know "passport size". Ask for 3.5 × 4.5 cm on a white background, on photo paper, not a computer printout. Studios usually print several on one sheet.
- **In the US:** some pharmacy chains list India among the countries they make photos for. Walgreens, for example, includes India on its list; ask in store, and say whether the photo is for a passport (35 × 45 mm, white) or a visa (2 × 2 inches). See our guide to [places that take visa photos](/tool/visa-photo-near-me).
- **For OCI:** you need a square digital file on a light background that is not white, which most store photos are not. The simplest route is to take the photo yourself and use the OCI setting below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Indian passport photo size in cm?

3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm, about 1.38 × 1.77 inches), in colour, on a plain white background, with the face filling most of the frame. At 300 dpi that is 413 × 531 pixels.

### Do I need to bring photos to the Passport Seva Kendra?

No, unless the applicant is under 4. The PSK takes your photo at the appointment. Bring a 4.5 × 3.5 cm photo on a white background for a child under 4.

### What is the OCI photo size in pixels?

Square, between 200 × 200 and 900 × 900 pixels, JPEG, no more than 200 KB.

### Can the OCI photo have a white background?

No. The OCI portal asks for a plain light-coloured background that is not white.

### What is the photo size for an Indian e-Visa?

There is no printed size. Upload a square JPEG between 10 KB and 1 MB, with a plain light or white background and no glasses.
