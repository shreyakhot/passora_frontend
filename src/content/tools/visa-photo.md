## US visa photo requirements

These rules apply to US nonimmigrant visas (tourist, student, work) and immigrant visas. A consular officer makes the final decision on whether your photo is acceptable.

### Size and digital specs

Most applicants upload a digital photo with the online DS-160 form. You need a printed photo only if the upload fails or the embassy asks for one.

| | Digital photo (DS-160 upload) | Printed photo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Size | Square, 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels | 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) |
| Head size | 50–69% of the image height, chin to top of head | 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) |
| File | JPEG, 240 kB or smaller, colour (sRGB) | Photo-quality paper, matte or glossy |

The State Department's own cropping tool saves photos at 600 × 600 pixels. If your file is too large, reduce the quality setting rather than the pixel size, and keep the photo square. For the printed format, our [guide to 2 × 2 inch photos](/tool/2x2-passport-photo) covers sizing and printing.

### Background

Plain **white or off-white**, with no pattern, no shadows and nothing else in the frame. Stand a little away from the wall so your head does not cast a shadow on it.

### Glasses

**No glasses.** The State Department no longer allows eyeglasses in new visa photos. The only exception is when they cannot be removed for medical reasons, and then you need a statement signed by a medical professional. Tinted or dark lenses are never allowed.

### Recent photo

The photo must be taken in the **last 6 months** and show how you look now. Also:

- Face the camera directly, with a neutral expression and both eyes open.
- Wear the clothes you normally wear every day. Uniforms are not allowed, except religious clothing worn daily.
- No hats or head coverings, except for religious reasons. Your full face must be visible, and the covering must not cast shadows on it.
- Do not edit the photo. The State Department says photos must not be digitally altered.

## Visa photo size by destination

Every country sets its own size, and many ask for a different shape from the US square. These are the formats for the most requested destinations; each has a full guide.

| Destination | Printed size | Digital upload | Background |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| United States | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) | Square JPEG, 600–1200 px, max 240 kB | White or off-white |
| Schengen countries | 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm) | Often taken at the visa centre | White or light-coloured (varies by country) |
| Canada (visitor visa) | At least 35 × 45 mm, head 31–36 mm | Per the online application | Plain white or light-coloured |
| India (e-Visa) | Not needed | Square JPEG, 10 kB to 1 MB | Plain light-coloured or white |

Details for each:

- **Schengen:** the [photo size for a Schengen visa](/tool/schengen-eu-photo-size), including face size and how each consulate handles photos.
- **Canada:** visitor visas, permanent residence and citizenship photos are covered in our [guide to Canadian photo rules](/tool/canadian-passport-photos).
- **India:** e-Visa, regular visa and OCI photos are in our [guide to Indian passport, visa and OCI photos](/tool/indian-passport-photo-oci-photo).
- **Other countries:** see our [list of photo sizes for every country](/tool/passport-photo-size-by-country).

Always check the embassy or the visa application centre for your country before you print. Some centres take your photo on site, and some accept only photos that meet their local rules.

## How to upload a visa photo online

For the US DS-160:

1. Get a photo that meets the rules above, either from a store or studio as a digital file or taken at home against a plain white or off-white wall.
2. Make sure the file is square, JPEG, 600 × 600 pixels or larger and no more than 240 kB.
3. In the DS-160, upload the photo when the form asks for it. The photo tool checks the file before accepting it.
4. If the upload fails, finish the form anyway and bring one printed 2 × 2 inch photo to your interview, with the DS-160 confirmation page.

Do not crop a group photo, scan an old print or use a selfie taken at arm's length. Our [guide to digital passport and visa photo files](/tool/digital-passport-photo) explains how to check pixel size and file size on a phone or computer.

## Common visa photo rejections

| Problem | How to avoid it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Wearing glasses | Take them off, even if you wear them every day |
| Shadows on the face or background | Face a window or use two light sources; stand away from the wall |
| Head too big or too small | Head 50–69% of the image height (25–35 mm in print) |
| Wrong shape or size | Square for the US; check the size for other countries |
| Old photo | Take a new one if it is more than 6 months old |
| Edited or filtered | Upload the original photo; crop it but do not retouch it |
| Low quality or blurry | Use a camera or phone in good light, not a scan or screenshot |

The State Department's photo checklist for passports uses the same basic rules; see our [checklist of photo rules](/tool/passport-photo-requirements).

## Make a visa photo online

You can take the photo yourself and prepare it at home:

1. Stand against a plain white or off-white wall, facing a window or another even light.
2. Ask someone to take the photo from about 4 feet (1.2 m) away, at eye level, with no glasses and a neutral expression.
3. Use the [online photo maker](/tool/online-passport-photo-maker) to crop the photo to the size for your destination: a square file for the DS-160, or a print sheet for countries that want paper photos.

For a US visa, the photo should need only cropping and sizing, because the State Department does not accept altered photos. If you would rather have the photo taken for you, see our guide to [places that take visa photos](/tool/visa-photo-near-me).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size is a US visa photo?

A printed US visa photo is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). A digital photo for the DS-160 is square, between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels, in JPEG format and no larger than 240 kB.

### Can I use my passport photo for a visa?

For a US visa, only if it meets the visa rules: taken in the last 6 months, without glasses, on a white or off-white background. For another country, use a photo made to that country's size, which is often 35 × 45 mm rather than square.

### Can I wear glasses in a visa photo?

Not for a US visa, unless they cannot be removed for medical reasons and you include a signed medical statement. Other countries have their own rules; Canada, for example, allows clear prescription glasses if the eyes are fully visible.

### What happens if my DS-160 photo upload fails?

You can still submit the DS-160. Bring one printed 2 × 2 inch photo that meets the requirements to your visa interview.

### How recent must a visa photo be?

The US asks for a photo taken in the last 6 months. Schengen countries and Canada set the same limit for visa photos.
