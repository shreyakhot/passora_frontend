Site Architecture: Clusters & Linking Rules

A. Core & Local | 8 pages | 5408450 searches/mo | Hub: passport-photo
Purpose: Head terms, local intent, price and speed. The pillar page owns "passport photo" and routes readers to spokes.
Linking: Every page links up to /passport-photo/. Local pages (near-me, photographer, size-near-me, visa-near-me) cross-link only as "looking for X instead?" boxes, never with the other page's primary keyword as the anchor.

B. Retailer Brands | 9 pages | 721250 searches/mo | Hub: passport-photos-near-me
Purpose: One page per retailer/brand so branded queries never compete with generic pages.
Linking: Each brand page links up to /passport-photos-near-me/ and /passport-photo-cost/, across to 2 alternatives, and to /online-passport-photo-maker/ as the no-trip option. Generic pages link DOWN to brand pages from comparison tables.

C. DIY, Online & Tools | 7 pages | 134550 searches/mo | Hub: online-passport-photo-maker
Purpose: DIY method and tool pages that feed conversions on the product page.
Linking: All DIY/tool pages carry a contextual CTA to /online-passport-photo-maker/ (the only money page). Comparison page links to it as the recommended option.

D. Size & Requirements | 9 pages | 658150 searches/mo | Hub: passport-photo-requirements
Purpose: Size, format and compliance references that answer pre-purchase questions.
Linking: Size pages form a triangle: /passport-photo-size/ (concept) <-> /2x2-passport-photo/ <-> /35x45mm-passport-photo/. Requirements <-> good-photo tips link once each, with descriptive anchors.

E. Visa, Immigration & Countries | 7 pages | 109150 searches/mo | Hub: visa-photo
Purpose: Destination-specific rules and immigration use cases.
Linking: Each country page links up to /visa-photo/ or /passport-photo-size-by-country/ and across to the matching size format (2x2 or 35x45). Promote hub sections to standalone pages when volume justifies.

SHEET: Notes & Flags
================================================================================
How keywords were mapped: Each keyword was assigned to exactly ONE page using ordered rules (brand > country > format > intent). One primary keyword per page; all other keywords on that page are supporting. Synonyms/spelling variants (e.g., "2x2", "2 by 2", "2*2") are consolidated so Google sees one page per intent.

Volume data caveat: Search volumes in the export are bucketed (50 / 500 / 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000), typical of a Keyword Planner account without active ad spend. "Est. total searches" sums those buckets, so treat it as an order-of-magnitude ranking, not a forecast. Validate priorities with a second tool (Semrush, Ahrefs, Search Console).

Rite Aid: All Rite Aid stores have closed (confirmed by CBS/CNN reports, Oct 2025). Page 16 is written as a short "closed - go here instead" page; do not publish prices or hours.

Retailer facts: Prices, hours and which locations offer passport photos change often. Verify every brand-page claim on the retailer's own site before publishing; outlines are structured as questions for that reason.

Official requirements: Photo size, pixel and file specs vary by country and change. Validate every requirements page against the issuing authority (e.g., US State Department, IRCC, USCIS, VFS/embassy sites).

Ambiguous / low-value keywords: "rimowa passport photos" (luggage brand, mapped to page 17), "passport phone" (mapped to page 19), "pp size / pp photo" (assumed Indian shorthand for passport photo, page 38), "passport photo orchard" and "passport photos new plymouth" (Singapore / NZ place names, mapped to page 39), "passport photo spadina" (Toronto, page 37). Review or drop if you are not targeting those markets.

Non-English variants: "foto passport", "foto pasaporte near me", "pass foto near me", "foto visa schengen" are mapped to English pages with short Spanish sections. If Spanish-speaking US traffic is a priority, build a dedicated /es/ page with hreflang.

Near-me pages: Near-me pages need real, crawlable location content (city pages, a locator, LocalBusiness/Place schema where appropriate) or they will underperform. Four near-me pages are deliberately separated by intent: generic locator, professional/studio, passport-SIZE print shops, visa.

Cannibalization safeguards: (1) One primary keyword per page, none repeated. (2) Brand pages own every query containing that brand. (3) Size pages split by format: concept / 2x2 / 35x45 mm / by-country. (4) "Best" roundups split by scope: cross-method vs online-only brands. (5) Cost head terms live on one page; brand cost terms live on brand pages.

Site-type assumption: No business details were provided, so pages assume a passport photo brand with an online photo tool (the money page is /online-passport-photo-maker/). Swap the CTAs if you are a studio, a retailer, or an affiliate publisher.
